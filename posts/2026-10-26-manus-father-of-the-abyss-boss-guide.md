**Manus, Father of the Abyss** is the final boss of the DLC: the ancient man at the root of the Abyss, the darkness that took over Artorias and Oolacile. He swings wildly with a huge hand and a catalyst, and **uses dark magic once he's lost about 40% of his health. Carry the Silver Pendant.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Chasm of the Abyss, the end of Oolacile Township |
| **Help** | **Sif's summon sign** is near the arena if you rescued young Sif earlier in the DLC |
| **Reward** | **Soul of Manus**, and **Dusk of Oolacile** is saved |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +15, 40 Vitality, a greatshield helps |

## His attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Hand slams | Big slams and grabs with his free hand | Roll toward him and to the side |
| Wild combo | A fast chain of swipes | Back off or roll through; attack after |
| Dark orbs | Many dark orbs that chase you (below ~60% health) | Use the **Silver Pendant** to block them, or run until they burst |
| Dark blast | A big burst of dark around him | Roll away |

## Strategy

1. **Summon Sif** if the sign is there. It takes some of his attention.
2. **Stay close.** Many of his attacks are easier to dodge up close.
3. **When the dark orbs start, use the Silver Pendant.** It blocks the orbs. Without it, run sideways in a circle until they burst.
4. **A greatshield** makes his slams much safer.
5. **Attack after combos only.** One or two hits, then roll.
