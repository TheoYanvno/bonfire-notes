The Bell Gargoyles guard the **first Bell of Awakening** on the roof of the Undead Parish church. It starts as one gargoyle, and partway through a **second one joins**. The whole fight is about killing the first one quickly so you're never fighting two at full health.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The church roof in the Undead Parish |
| **Bosses** | Two Bell Gargoyles (the second joins partway through) |
| **Reward** | Souls, the first Bell of Awakening, and a Gargoyle Tail Axe if you cut a tail |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +5, Estus kindled if possible, Solaire summoned |

## Get help: summon Solaire

If you talked to **Solaire** on the stairs before the dragon bridge, you can summon him for this fight. You need to be in **human form** (use a Humanity at a bonfire). Look for his glowing summon sign near the fog gate and use it. He distracts one gargoyle and adds damage.

## Its attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Halberd swing | Wide sweep with its spear-axe | Roll toward and past it |
| Tail swipe | Spins and sweeps its tail | Stay at its side, not behind |
| Dive | Flies up and swoops down at you | Roll sideways as it comes in |
| Fire breath | The **second** gargoyle breathes fire in a cone | Get behind it or far away |

## Strategy

1. **Rush the first gargoyle** and hit hard while it's alone. Every bit of damage now saves you later.
2. **Aim for its tail.** A strong hit to the tail cuts it off and drops the **Gargoyle Tail Axe**, a good early axe.
3. **When the second appears,** focus the hurt one. Keep both gargoyles on the same side of you so you can see both.
4. **Watch for the fire breath** from the second gargoyle. It covers a wide area.
5. **Stay away from the edges** of the roof.

## Build tips

- **Strength:** two-handed heavy hits stagger them. Good for tail cutting.
- **Dexterity:** chain fast hits while it recovers from swings.
- **Magic and pyromancy:** fight from the far side of the roof, and cast while Solaire keeps one busy.

## After the fight

Climb the bell tower and **ring the bell.** One down, one to go. Next boss: [Capra Demon](capra-demon-boss-guide.html).
