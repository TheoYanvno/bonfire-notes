**The Duke's Archives** is the library of **Seath the Scaleless**, the dragon who betrayed his own kind. It's full of moving staircases, crystal golems and channelers. Beyond it is the **Crystal Cave**, where the bridges are invisible. Seath holds one of the four Lord Souls.

| | |
|---|---|
| **How to get in** | The elevator in Anor Londo, after placing the Lordvessel |
| **Biggest danger** | Channelers who buff enemies, crystal golems, invisible paths |
| **Bring** | A light ranged attack (bow or spells) for the channelers |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +10 to +15 |
| **Goal** | Destroy Seath's crystal and beat him |

## 1. The library

The Archives are tall halls of bookshelves. **Channelers**, robed sorcerers, stand at the edges. They throw magic and power up nearby enemies. **Kill them first.**

Some staircases **move** when you pull a lever. If a stair doesn't lead anywhere, look for the lever that changes it.

## 2. The fight you lose

Partway through, you meet **Seath** for the first time. **You can't beat him yet:** a crystal is keeping him alive. Let the fight end. You wake up in a **prison tower**.

## 3. The prison tower

- **Find the way out of your cell.** The key is close by.
- **Prison guards** with bells walk the halls. Their bells summon monsters and raise an alarm. **Kill the guards quietly** or avoid them.
- **Big Hat Logan**, the sorcerer from Sen's Fortress, may be studying here. He's changed.

## 4. Back through the Archives

Escape the tower and work back through the library. You'll find more channelers, crystal golems and a secret or two behind bookshelves. Look for a **bonfire** near the gardens outside.

## 5. The Crystal Cave

Past the gardens is the **Crystal Cave**: glowing crystal formations and dark drops.

- **The bridges are invisible.** Look for the **sparkles** falling from the ceiling; they land on the hidden paths. Walk where they land.
- **Crystal golems** guard the paths. Fight them on solid ground.

## 6. Seath, for real

At the end of the cave, Seath waits beside the **Primordial Crystal** that kept him alive. Read the [Seath boss guide](seath-the-scaleless-boss-guide.html): break the crystal first, and he's mortal.

## Tips

- **Channelers first, always.**
- **Losing the first Seath fight is the plan.** Don't waste Estus on it.
- **Sorcerers:** cut Seath's tail for the **Moonlight Greatsword**.
