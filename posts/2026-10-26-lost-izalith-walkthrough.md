**Lost Izalith** was the city of the Witch of Izalith, before her attempt to copy the First Flame went wrong and created the demons. Now it's a lava lake with ruined towers sticking out of it. You need the **Orange Charred Ring** from the Centipede Demon to cross it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **How to get in** | The sealed door past the Centipede Demon, once the Lordvessel is placed |
| **Biggest danger** | Lava (without the ring), huge dragon-legged demons, chaos bugs |
| **Bring** | **Orange Charred Ring**, a weapon at +10 to +15 |
| **Goal** | Beat the Bed of Chaos for its Lord Soul |

## 1. The lava field

The first area is a wide lava field. **Wear the Orange Charred Ring.** Even with it, lava hurts a little, so don't wander.

**Huge creatures** with dragon legs and no upper bodies stomp across the field. They're slow but hit like trucks. **Run past them** along the edges, or fight them only where you have room to dodge.

## 2. The bonfire

On the far side, a passage leads to a **bonfire** and a shortcut back toward the Demon Ruins. From now on, Lost Izalith is a short trip.

## 3. Chaos bugs and Titanite Demons

Inside the ruined city:

- **Chaos bugs** (white, eyeless creatures) crawl on the floors and lunge. Kill them one at a time.
- **Titanite Demons** guard some halls. They're tough, but they drop titanite.
- **Look up and around.** Some of the best items are on side paths.

## 4. Solaire

If you've followed **Solaire's** story, you may find him here, near a glowing bug on someone's head. What happens next depends on what you did earlier. It's one of the saddest moments in the game.

## 5. The Bed of Chaos

At the heart of the city, in a huge root-filled chamber, is the **Bed of Chaos**, the twisted remains of the Witch of Izalith. It's a puzzle more than a fight. Read the [Bed of Chaos boss guide](bed-of-chaos-boss-guide.html). It drops a **Lord Soul**.

## Tips

- **Keep the Orange Charred Ring on** whenever you're near lava.
- **Don't fight the dragon-legged demons in the open.** Go around.
- **Find the shortcut bonfire first.** It makes every attempt at the Bed of Chaos quick.
