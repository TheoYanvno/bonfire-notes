The **Hunter** is the only class that starts with a **bow**. It also gets a Shortsword, a Large Leather Shield and Dexterity 14. This guide builds a **Dexterity archer**: snipe dangerous enemies before they reach you, then switch to a fast sword.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting level** | 4 |
| **Stats** | Vitality 11 · Attunement 9 · Endurance 11 · Strength 12 · Dexterity 14 · Resistance 11 · Intelligence 9 · Faith 9 |
| **Weapons** | Shortsword and a Short Bow, with a stack of Standard Arrows |
| **Shield** | Large Leather Shield |
| **Armor** | The Leather set (armor, gloves, boots; no helmet) |
| **Plays like** | Pull enemies one at a time with arrows, then fight them in a doorway |

> **In one line:** a sword fighter who never has to walk into an ambush blind.

## What the Hunter is good at

- **Pulling.** One arrow brings one enemy to you instead of a whole group.
- **Sniping casters and archers** who would otherwise wear you down from a ledge.
- **The Drake Sword.** Shoot the red drake's tail from under the Undead Burg bridge until it falls off, and you get the Drake Sword, a strong early weapon. The Hunter can do this from the start.

## What to level up

| Stat | Early (to the Taurus Demon) | Mid (to the Gaping Dragon) | Late (Anor Londo) | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vitality | 16 | 25 | 30 | 40 |
| Endurance | 15 | 22 | 30 | 35 |
| Dexterity | 16 | 22 | 30 | 40 |
| Strength | 14 | 16 | 20 | 20 |
| Everything else | leave it | leave it | leave it | leave it |

- **Strength 20 and Dexterity 20** are what the big bows need, including the Dragonslayer Greatbow later on.
- **Dexterity raises bow damage** as well as sword damage, so it pays off twice.

## Weapons

| Weapon | Where | Why use it |
|---|---|---|
| Shortsword | Starting gear | Light and quick. Fine through the first areas. |
| Short Bow | Starting gear | Pulls and snipes. Restock arrows from the Undead Burg merchant. |
| Drake Sword | Shoot or cut the tail off the red drake on the Undead Burg bridge (from below) | A big early damage jump with a ranged wave attack. |
| Black Bow of Pharis | Dropped by Pharis, the archer in the Darkroot Garden woods | Faster draw and more damage than the Short Bow. |
| Dragonslayer Greatbow | Dropped by the Silver Knight archers in Anor Londo | Huge arrows that knock enemies off their feet. Needs high Strength and Dexterity. |
| Balder Side Sword | Balder Knights, Undead Parish | A strong Dexterity melee weapon to pair with the bow. |

## Armor, shield and rings

| Slot | Early | Later |
|---|---|---|
| Armor | Leather set | Mid-weight pieces; stay under 50% equip load |
| Shield | Large Leather Shield | Grass Crest Shield (Darkroot Basin) |
| Rings | Hornet Ring (behind Artorias's grave): bigger backstabs | Havel's Ring if you want heavier armor |

## How to play it

1. **Always open with an arrow.** Shoot the enemy you want, then back up to a doorway and fight it there.
2. **Aim manually** (hold the bow button to zoom) for long shots and weak points.
3. **Swap to melee early.** Most fights still end with a sword, so keep your melee weapon upgraded first.
4. **Carry a stack of arrows.** Running out mid-dungeon is the Hunter's only real weakness.
