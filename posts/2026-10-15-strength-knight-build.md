The Strength Knight is the most forgiving way to play Dark Souls. You wear heavy armor, carry a big shield, and swing weapons that knock enemies off their feet. If you are new to the series, start here.

> **In one line:** block, wait for an opening, hit hard once, block again.

## Starting class

Pick the **Knight**. It starts with the most health of any class, a Longsword, a sturdy Tower Kite Shield and full armor. The Warrior is a fine second choice if you prefer to dodge more and block less.

Any class can become this build eventually, because every level-up point goes where you choose. The Knight just makes the first five hours much gentler.

## Where to spend your level-up points

Each time you level up at a bonfire, you get one point. Spend them in this order:

| Stat | Early game (to the Taurus Demon) | Mid game (to the Gaping Dragon) | Late game | Final goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vitality (health) | 16 | 25 | 30 | 40 |
| Endurance (stamina, equip load) | 16 | 25 | 30 | 40 |
| Strength (damage, heavy weapons) | 16 | 24 | 32 | 40 |
| Dexterity | leave it | leave it | 12–14 | 14 |
| Attunement, Intelligence, Faith, Resistance | leave them | leave them | leave them | leave them |

**Why this order:**

- **Strength 16 early** lets you use the Claymore. Strength 24 lets you one-hand the Zweihander, but two-handing counts your Strength as 1.5×, so 16 Strength is enough to swing it with both hands.
- **Vitality and Endurance are your real defense.** More health means fewer deaths; more stamina means more swings and more blocks before your guard breaks.
- **40 is the soft cap.** Past 40 Strength, each point adds much less damage. Endurance stops raising stamina at 40 (it still raises equip load). Vitality gives the most health per point up to 30, less up to 50.
- **Skip Resistance.** It adds very little. Armor does its job better.

## Weapons

| Weapon | Where | Needs | Why use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Longsword | Knight starting gear | Str 10, Dex 10 | Reliable, fast enough, upgrades well. Fine for the whole first area. |
| Claymore | Undead Burg, near the bridge watched by the red dragon | Str 16, Dex 10 | Wide sweeps and a lunging thrust. Great crowd control. |
| Zweihander | Firelink Shrine graveyard (watch for skeletons) | Str 24, Dex 10 | Huge damage and stagger. Two-hand it with 16 Strength early. |
| Black Knight Sword | Rare drop from Black Knights | Str 20, Dex 18 | One of the best Strength weapons in the game, if it drops. |

**Upgrades:** Andre the blacksmith in the Undead Parish upgrades weapons with Titanite. On the normal path you use Titanite Shards (+1 to +5), Large Titanite Shards (+6 to +10), Titanite Chunks (+11 to +14) and a Titanite Slab (+15). One well-upgraded weapon beats five unupgraded ones, so pick a favorite and commit.

## Armor and shield

| Slot | Early | Later |
|---|---|---|
| Armor | Knight set (starting gear) | Elite Knight set (Darkroot Garden); Havel's set if you defeat Havel the Rock |
| Shield | Tower Kite Shield (starting gear) | Grass Crest Shield (Darkroot Garden): faster stamina recovery |

**Equip load matters.** Watch the percentage on your equipment screen:

- **Under 25%:** fast roll.
- **25–50%:** normal roll. This is the sweet spot for this build.
- **Over 50%:** slow "fat roll". Avoid it.

If heavy armor pushes you past 50%, raise Endurance or swap a piece for something lighter.

## Rings

- **Havel's Ring:** raises your equip load by 50%, so you can wear heavier armor and still roll normally. Havel the Rock drops it. He hides behind a locked door at the bottom of a watchtower in the Undead Burg. He is very tough, so come back later.
- **Ring of Favor and Protection:** more health, stamina and equip load, found later in the game. The catch is that the ring breaks if you ever take it off.

## How to play it

1. **Shield up while walking** into new rooms. Most surprise attacks bounce off.
2. **Wait for the enemy's swing to finish,** then hit once or twice. Greedy third hits get you killed.
3. **Two-hand big weapons** against enemies that don't hit hard, to deal more damage and stagger them.
4. **Kick** (forward + attack) shields out of the way against enemies that block.

When you are ready, read the [Asylum Demon](asylum-demon-boss-guide.html) guide. With this build it is a straightforward first boss fight.
