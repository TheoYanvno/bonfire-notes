The **Sorcerer** fights from a distance with magic. It starts with Intelligence 15, Attunement 15 and the **Soul Arrow** spell, but also the lowest health and stamina. This guide builds a **pure sorcerer**: fragile early, then one of the strongest characters in the game.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting level** | 3 |
| **Stats** | Vitality 8 · Attunement 15 · Endurance 8 · Strength 9 · Dexterity 11 · Resistance 8 · Intelligence 15 · Faith 8 |
| **Weapon** | Dagger |
| **Catalyst** | Sorcerer's Catalyst (hold it to cast) |
| **Spell** | Soul Arrow |
| **Shield** | Small Leather Shield |
| **Armor** | The Sorcerer set (hat, cloak, gauntlets, boots) |
| **Plays like** | Keep your distance, shoot, back away, shoot again |

> **In one line:** the hardest first hour, and the easiest last ten.

## What the Sorcerer is good at

- **Range.** Many enemies die before they reach you.
- **Bosses.** Several big bosses are much easier from a distance.
- **The downside:** Vitality 8 and Endurance 8. Early on, everything can kill you in two hits.

## What to level up

| Stat | Early (to the Taurus Demon) | Mid (to the Gaping Dragon) | Late (Anor Londo) | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vitality | 15 | 22 | 30 | 35 |
| Intelligence | 18 | 24 | 32 | 40 |
| Attunement | 15 | 16 | 19 | 24 |
| Endurance | 12 | 16 | 20 | 25 |
| Strength / Dexterity | leave them | leave them | leave them | only for a backup weapon |

- **Intelligence makes every spell stronger**, up to a soft cap of 40.
- **Attunement adds spell slots** at 10, 12, 14, 16, 19, 23, 28 and more. You start with a lot; add more only when you run out of slots.
- **Don't skip Vitality.** Even a sorcerer gets hit.

## Spells and where to learn them

| Spell | From | Why use it |
|---|---|---|
| Soul Arrow | Starting spell | Your main attack for the first areas. |
| Heavy Soul Arrow, Great Soul Arrow | **Griggs of Vinheim** (free him in the Lower Undead Burg with the Residence Key; he then moves to Firelink Shrine) | More damage, or more casts. |
| Magic Weapon | Griggs | Turns your weapon into a magic one for a while. Great with a backup sword. |
| Soul Spear, Homing Soulmass | **Big Hat Logan** (free him from a cage in Sen's Fortress; he later teaches in the Duke's Archives) | Your strongest attacks for the rest of the game. |

**Moonlight Greatsword:** cut off Seath the Scaleless's tail in the Duke's Archives. It's a sword that scales with Intelligence and fires a magic wave.

## Armor, shield and rings

| Slot | Early | Later |
|---|---|---|
| Armor | Sorcerer set | Something light with better physical defense; stay under 25% equip load |
| Shield | Small Leather Shield | Any light shield; you'll rarely block |
| Rings | Dragoncrest rings (sold by the sorcery trainers): stronger spells | Havel's Ring if you want heavier armor without slowing down |

## How to play it

1. **Distance is your armor.** Back away while enemies close in, and cast when they're in range.
2. **Carry a melee weapon** for enemies that resist magic, and upgrade it a little.
3. **Lock on** to make spells home in.
4. **Ration casts.** Each spell has a limited number of uses until you rest.
