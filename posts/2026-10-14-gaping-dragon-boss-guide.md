The Gaping Dragon is the first truly huge boss. It has a lot of health and its acid melts your equipment. But it is slow to turn, and most of its attacks only cover the space in front of it. **Fight from its side and back.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Depths |
| **Weak to** | Patient attacks to its legs and tail; its attacks are slow |
| **Reward** | **Blighttown Key** and souls; cut its tail for the **Dragon King Greataxe** |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +5 to +7, 25 Vitality, several Estus Flasks, a spare weapon |

## Its attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Charge | Runs forward in a straight line | Roll sideways out of its path |
| Belly flop | Rears up on its back legs, then slams its whole body down | Run away when it rears up; this is its strongest attack |
| Acid vomit | Spews acid in front of it | Stay at its side; acid wears down your equipment |
| Tail swipe | Sweeps its tail behind it | Watch when you attack from behind |
| Bite grab | Lunges with its huge mouth | Never stand in front of it |

## Strategy

1. **Start at its side.** Attack its back legs. It is slow to turn toward you.
2. **When it rears up, run.** The belly flop covers a wide area around it.
3. **After a charge,** it stops for a moment. That's your best window to hit it.
4. **Heal far away** from it, behind it if possible.
5. **Watch your weapon's durability.** Acid wears it down. Carry a backup weapon.

## Cutting the tail

Hit the tail with heavy attacks to cut it off. It drops the **Dragon King Greataxe**, a giant weapon with very high Strength requirements. You won't use it soon, but it's a nice trophy for Strength builds.

## After the fight

The **Blighttown Key** opens the way to Blighttown and the second Bell of Awakening. You've now beaten five bosses. Head back to the [guides hub](lordran-guide-hub.html) for what comes next.
