**Gwyn, Lord of Cinder** is the god who started the Age of Fire and then gave himself to the flame to keep it burning. What's left is a burnt-out shell that still fights like a demon. **He's aggressive and relentless. He can also be parried.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Kiln of the First Flame, the end of the game |
| **Resists** | Fire |
| **Reward** | **Soul of Gwyn**, then the choice of ending |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +12 to +15, 35+ Vitality |

## His attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Sword combos | Long chains of fiery slashes | Roll **toward** him and to the side; count the swings |
| Leaping slam | Jumps and brings the sword down | Roll sideways at the last moment |
| Grab | Grabs you and burns you | Don't stay right in front of him after a combo |
| Charge | Rushes forward | Roll through it |

## Strategy

1. **The parry option:** his combos are parryable. If you can parry, wait for a swing, parry it and riposte. Repeat. This is the easiest way to win.
2. **Without parrying:** **stay close** and roll through his swings to his side. After a combo ends, hit him once or twice.
3. **Don't heal close to him.** He's fast. Back off far before using Estus.
4. **Stamina is everything.** Leave enough to roll.

## After the fight

A bonfire appears. Resting at it **links the fire**; walking away lets it **fade**. Read [The Age of Fire, Explained](age-of-fire-explained.html) for what each ending means. Back to the [guides hub](lordran-guide-hub.html).
