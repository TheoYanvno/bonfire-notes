The **Four Kings** were the rulers of New Londo, given Lord Souls and then swallowed by the Abyss. You fight them in an endless black void. It's a **damage race**: a new king joins every so often, so **the faster you kill each one, the fewer you face at once.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Abyss, at the bottom of drained New Londo |
| **Must have** | **Covenant of Artorias** ring, or the Abyss kills you instantly |
| **Reward** | **Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard** (for the Lordvessel) |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +12 to +15, 35 Vitality, high magic defense |

## Their attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Sword swings | Wide, slow slashes | Block or roll through |
| Grab | Reaches out, grabs you and drains your health | Roll back when it lunges with an open hand |
| Magic blasts | Homing magic missiles from range | Stay close; they mostly use magic when you're far away |
| Explosion | A dark burst around the king | Back off when it glows |

## Strategy

1. **Wear the Covenant of Artorias**, then drop in.
2. **Walk forward until the first king appears** and rush it.
3. **Stay close and keep hitting.** Two-handing a heavy weapon does the most damage.
4. **Ignore your stamina pride.** Block when you must, but your goal is damage.
5. **Use Estus between kings** if you can. Ideally you kill each one before the next arrives.
6. **Havel's Ring and heavy armor** let you trade hits, which helps in a race like this.

## After the fight

The serpent **Kaathe** appears and talks to you about the Lord Souls. What he says matters for the ending. Back to the [guides hub](lordran-guide-hub.html).
