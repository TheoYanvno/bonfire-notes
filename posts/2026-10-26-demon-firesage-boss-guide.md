The **Demon Firesage** looks a lot like the first boss of the game, the Asylum Demon, but covered in fire. It swings a burning hammer and sets off fire blasts around itself. **Stay behind it and hit its back**, the same idea that worked in the Asylum.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Demon Ruins, the fog gate in the middle of the ruins |
| **Resists** | Fire. Use your weapon, not pyromancy |
| **Reward** | Souls |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +10 to +12, 30 Vitality, fire resistance |

## Its attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Hammer swings | Heavy swings in front of it | Stay behind it |
| Fire blast | Raises the hammer, then a burst of fire around it | **Run away** when it lifts the hammer high |
| Butt slam | Jumps and lands on you | Get out from under it |
| Fire breath | Fire in a cone in front | Move to its side |

## Strategy

1. **Get behind it.** Most of its attacks go forward.
2. **Two or three hits on its tail or legs**, then step back.
3. **When the hammer goes up, run.** The fire blast covers a wide circle.
4. **Fire-resistant armor or a ring** takes the edge off its attacks.

## After the fight

Keep going down the ruins toward the **Centipede Demon**, which has the ring you need for Lost Izalith ([Boss Guide 18](centipede-demon-boss-guide.html)). Back to the [guides hub](lordran-guide-hub.html).
