The **Iron Golem** guards the roof of Sen's Fortress. It's enormous and hits hard, but it's slow, its legs are an easy target, and the arena has no railings. **Hit its legs until it stumbles; if it's near the edge, it can fall off the roof.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Sen's Fortress, the fog gate at the top of the fortress |
| **Reward** | **Core of an Iron Golem**, then a ride to Anor Londo |
| **Help** | **Black Iron Tarkus** can be summoned outside the fog gate (if you meet the requirements, his sign is there) |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +7 to +10, 25 Vitality, decent poise |

## Its attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Overhead slam | Raises its axe high, then brings it down | Roll sideways, toward its legs |
| Wide sweep | Swings its axe across the front | Stay behind its legs, or roll through |
| Grab and throw | Reaches down with its free hand | Roll away when it leans down; it can throw you off the roof |
| Shockwave | Stomps and sends out a blast | Keep your distance when it raises its leg |

## Strategy

1. **Stay at its legs**, slightly behind. Most attacks hit the front.
2. **Hit the same leg** again and again. Enough damage knocks it to one knee and gives you free hits.
3. **Watch the edge.** You have no railing either. Don't roll toward the drop.
4. **Get it near the edge.** If it stumbles there, it can fall off and the fight ends.
5. **Summon Tarkus.** He's very strong and can nearly solo the Golem.

## After the fight

Walk to the edge where the golem stood. Bat-winged demons carry you to **Anor Londo**. See [Walkthrough Part 9](anor-londo-walkthrough.html). Back to the [guides hub](lordran-guide-hub.html).
