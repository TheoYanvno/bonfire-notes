**Darkroot** is a forest you can visit any time after the Undead Parish. None of it is required to finish the game, but it has some of the best gear for any build, a covenant, a boss you'll fight early and a boss you'll want to come back for. It comes in two parts: **Darkroot Garden**, behind Andre's forge, and **Darkroot Basin**, a lake valley below it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Biggest danger** | Tree monsters that grab you, Stone Knights, and the Hydra in the Basin |
| **Bring** | A weapon at +5 or higher; fire helps against the trees |
| **Recommended** | After the Bell Gargoyles; the woods past the sealed door much later |
| **Rewards** | Elite Knight armor, the Wolf Ring, the Divine Ember, the Grass Crest Shield |

## Darkroot Garden

### 1. Getting in

From **Andre's forge** in the Undead Parish, go down through the room with the **Titanite Demon**. You can run past it, or come back to kill it later: it drops titanite. Past it is a door into the garden.

### 2. Trees that are not trees

The forest is full of **walking trees**. Some stand still, some are half buried, and all of them can grab you. **Watch the shapes along the path**, and kill them one at a time. Fire works well.

### 3. The best loot

- **Elite Knight set:** on a corpse in an open area guarded by trees. Excellent armor for most builds.
- **Wolf Ring:** up some stairs past a Stone Knight. It raises poise, so small hits don't stagger you.
- **Partizan:** a spear in a small passage off the open area.
- **Enchanted Ember:** in a chest in a pool, guarded by two big mushroom people. They hit far harder than they look.

**Great Stone Knights** stand still until you get close. You can walk around most of them. If you fight one, hit it from behind and dodge its slow, heavy swings.

### 4. The Moonlight Butterfly

At the tower, a fog gate leads to the **Moonlight Butterfly**, a glowing creature that flies just out of reach and fires magic. Read the [Moonlight Butterfly boss guide](moonlight-butterfly-boss-guide.html). After it, climb the tower for the **Divine Ember**, which lets Andre upgrade weapons down the Divine path. Divine weapons stop skeletons in the Catacombs from getting back up.

### 5. The cat and the covenant

A big white cat named **Alvina** sits in the woods. Talk to her to join the **Forest Hunter** covenant, whose members defend the forest from invaders. A merchant named **Shiva of the East** waits nearby.

### 6. The sealed door

A large door with a glowing seal blocks part of the forest. It opens with the **Crest of Artorias**, which Andre sells. Behind it are more woods, a chest with the **Stone Knight set**, and the grave of the knight Artorias, guarded by **Great Grey Wolf Sif**. Come back much later; see the [Sif boss guide](great-grey-wolf-sif-boss-guide.html). Behind the grave is the **Hornet Ring**, which makes critical hits much stronger.

## Darkroot Basin

### 7. The lake

Darkroot Basin is a low valley with a lake. A ladder past a stone bridge in the Garden leads down, but it drops you near the **Hydra**, a many-headed serpent in the lake that spits water from range. **Avoid the water's edge** until you're ready, or fight it from close to the shore, where you can hit the heads.

### 8. Basin loot

- **Grass Crest Shield:** guarded by a **Black Knight** near the lowest part of the basin. It makes your stamina recover faster.
- **An alcove past the Hydra's lake** matters much later: it's the way into the Artorias of the Abyss DLC. See Part 19.

## Tips

- **Darkroot is optional.** If it feels too hard right after the Parish, come back after Sen's Fortress.
- **The Elite Knight set and the Grass Crest Shield** are worth the trip for nearly every build.
- **Stay away from the Hydra** until your weapon is at +8 or more.
