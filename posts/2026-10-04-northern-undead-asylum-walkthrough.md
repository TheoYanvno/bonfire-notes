The Northern Undead Asylum is Dark Souls' tutorial. It is short and safe, but it still teaches you the most important lesson in the game: **look around before you charge in.** This walkthrough takes you from your cell to the end of the area.

## 1. Escape the cell

You wake up in a cell with nothing but a broken sword hilt. Wait a moment: a knight looks down through a hole in the ceiling and drops a **corpse** into your cell. Loot it for the **Dungeon Cell Key** and open the door.

The messages on the floor explain the controls. Read them as you go.

## 2. The hallway and the first bonfire

Follow the corridor past a few hollows. They are weak and mostly harmless here. Climb the ladder and find the **bonfire**. Rest at it: resting refills your health and Estus but also brings most enemies back to life. That trade-off is the heart of Dark Souls.

## 3. Run from the Asylum Demon

Go through the large gate into the courtyard. A huge demon drops from above. **You are not supposed to fight it yet.** You have no real weapon.

Run straight for the **small open door on the left side of the courtyard.** A gate slams shut behind you. You are safe.

> If you do want to try fighting it now, you can, with the broken hilt. It takes a very long time. Escaping is the normal path.

## 4. Pick up your gear

Past the door is another bonfire. Rest, then follow the corridor. Your class's **shield and weapon** lie on corpses along this path. Equip them from your inventory right away.

The messages here teach you to lock on, attack, block and roll. Practice on the slow hollows.

## 5. The archer and the boulder

Climb the stairs toward the hollow archer at the top. **Careful:** a giant boulder comes rolling down the stairs at you. Step to the side and let it pass. It crashes through a wall at the bottom.

Go back down to look through the broken wall. A dying knight, **Oscar**, is sitting there. Talk to him. He gives you the **Estus Flask** (your healing potion) and the **Undead Asylum F2 East Key**, and tells you why you're here.

## 6. The ambush from above

Head back up and through the door on the upper floor. It leads to a balcony **above the Asylum Demon's courtyard.** The demon is waiting below.

Walk off the ledge and press attack while falling to land a **plunging attack.** It takes a big chunk of its health. Now fight it properly. See the [Asylum Demon boss guide](asylum-demon-boss-guide.html) for the full strategy.

## 7. Leave the Asylum

The demon drops the **Big Pilgrim's Key.** Rest at the bonfire, then use the key on the big double doors past the courtyard.

Walk outside to the cliff edge. A giant crow swoops down and carries you away to **Firelink Shrine** in Lordran. Next: [Walkthrough Part 2: Firelink Shrine](firelink-shrine-walkthrough.html).

## Tips for this area

- **Try every door.** The F2 East Key opens a side room with useful items.
- **Don't worry about dying.** You drop your souls where you die, and you can pick them up again if you get back there without dying first.
- **Choose a gift carefully** at character creation. A Master Key opens many shortcuts; Binoculars or a Pendant do almost nothing.
