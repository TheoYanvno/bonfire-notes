**Pinwheel** is a strange creature in a three-faced mask, the master of the necromancers in the Catacombs. He looks creepy and fights strangely, but he has very little health. **Rush him and keep hitting.** Most players beat him on the first try.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Catacombs, at the bottom of the tomb |
| **Reward** | **Rite of Kindling** (lets you kindle bonfires to hold more Estus) and souls |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +5 to +10 |

## His attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Fireballs | He throws small balls of fire | Sidestep, or block with a shield |
| Copies | He splits into two or three Pinwheels | The copies are weak; kill them or ignore them and chase the real one |
| Teleport | He blinks to another spot in the room | Head straight for him again |
| Big fire | A heavier fire attack when he's had time to charge | Don't let him have time: stay on him |

## Strategy

1. **Run at him the moment the fight starts.**
2. **Attack without stopping.** He doesn't hit back hard up close.
3. **When there are copies,** hit whichever is closest. Each copy dies in a hit or two.
4. **Don't stand back.** At range he has time to throw more fire.

## After the fight

The **Rite of Kindling** lets you kindle any bonfire, not just Fire Keeper bonfires, so it holds more Estus. Kindle the bonfires in the Tomb of the Giants, next door ([Part 14](tomb-of-the-giants-walkthrough.html)). Back to the [guides hub](lordran-guide-hub.html).
