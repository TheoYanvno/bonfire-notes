Deep in the golden city of Anor Londo waits the fight that ends more first playthroughs than any other: Dragon Slayer Ornstein, fast and agile with a spear, and Executioner Smough, a giant swinging a huge hammer. The trick is that you fight both at once.

## The big idea: split them up

Two bosses are hard because one is always attacking you while you deal with the other. Your goal is to keep them on the **same side** of you, so you only need to watch one direction.

- The room is lined with **pillars**. Ornstein darts around them, but Smough is big and slow, and the pillars block many of his swings.
- Keep moving in a wide circle so they stay grouped up in front of you.
- Attack only when one of them has finished a combo, then back off.

## Who to defeat first

When one of them falls, the other absorbs his power, heals to full and gets stronger for a second phase. This changes the fight:

- **Defeat Ornstein first** and Smough gets stronger, adding lightning to his hammer blows. Many players find this the easier order, because the second phase is a single slow giant.
- **Defeat Smough first** and Ornstein grows into a giant with a much bigger spear and new attacks.

Pick one target and stick with him. Splitting your damage between both just makes the fight longer.

## Getting help

If you have kept the friendly knight Solaire of Astora alive and you are human, you may be able to summon him for this fight. Other players can also join through summon signs. A second person to draw attention makes the fight far more manageable.

## Final tips

- Keep your equipment light enough to roll quickly. You'll be dodging a lot.
- Don't heal when one of them is closing in; back away behind a pillar first.
- If you lose, notice *what* killed you. Most deaths in this fight come from losing track of the second boss.

Whichever brother you defeat last drops his boss soul, which can later be turned into that boss's weapon. Take a breath, then go meet the princess.
