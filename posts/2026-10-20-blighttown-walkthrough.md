**Blighttown** has a reputation, and it earns it. You climb down a tower of rotten wooden walkways into a poisonous swamp. Enemies shoot poison darts from the dark, the walkways have no railings, and one wrong roll sends you falling. Take it slowly and it's very doable.

You reach it through the door the **Blighttown Key** opens, the key the [Gaping Dragon](gaping-dragon-boss-guide.html) drops in the Depths.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Biggest danger** | Toxic and poison from darts and the swamp |
| **Bring** | Purple Moss Clumps (cure poison), and a bow or throwing knives |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +5 or higher, 20+ Vitality |
| **Goal** | Reach the bottom, cross the swamp and ring the second Bell of Awakening |

> **Before you go:** stock up on **Purple Moss Clumps**. Poison drains your health slowly and toxic drains it fast. One clump cures both.

## 1. The upper walkways

Right past the door, the walkways are narrow and crowded with **infested ghouls**. They're weak alone and dangerous in groups. Fight them where the path is wide, never on a ledge.

**Blowdart snipers** sit in the shadows and shoot poison darts. When you see a dart hit you, look for the sniper and rush them. They are fragile up close.

## 2. Go down, carefully

The route down zigzags between platforms and ladders. Look before you roll: a lot of edges have no railing.

- **Use ladders, not drops.** Most drops look safe and aren't.
- **Pull enemies to you.** Throwing knives or a bow let you fight one at a time.
- **Look for the bonfire** about halfway down, on the walkways. Rest there before the swamp.

## 3. The swamp

At the bottom is a wide swamp of toxic water. Walking in it slows you down and poisons you over time, and giant mosquitoes swarm over it.

1. **Head straight for the far side** instead of exploring everything on your first trip.
2. **Cure poison when the bar turns green**, not when your health is already low.
3. **Kill mosquitoes as they come.** They are weak but they swarm.

### Maneater Mildred

A cannibal named **Maneater Mildred** may invade you in the swamp. She swings a huge butcher's knife. Back onto solid ground, let her swing first, then counterattack. If you don't kill her during the invasion, her summon sign appears later outside Quelaag's lair.

## 4. Quelaag's Domain

On the far side of the swamp, a cave leads to a fog gate. This is the lair of **Chaos Witch Quelaag**, half woman and half spider. Read the [Quelaag boss guide](chaos-witch-quelaag-boss-guide.html) before you go in.

## 5. The second bell

After Quelaag, take the stairs at the back of her lair up to the bell tower and ring the **second Bell of Awakening**. With both bells rung, the gate to **Sen's Fortress** opens, back up past the Undead Parish.

## Tips

- **There is another way in.** A second route reaches the bottom of Blighttown from the Valley of Drakes, but it takes much longer to get there.
- **Don't fight on ladders.** Climb down, clear the platform, then continue.
- **Poison resistance helps.** Armor with high poison resistance makes the swamp much less stressful.
- **Ring the bell, then rest.** You've earned it.
