Past Artorias lies **Oolacile Township**, a once-beautiful town now being consumed by the **Abyss**. Its people have turned into monsters. Below it, in the **Chasm of the Abyss**, is the source of it all.

| | |
|---|---|
| **How to get in** | Through the Royal Wood, past Knight Artorias |
| **Biggest danger** | Bloated sorcerers' dark magic, and darkness in the Chasm |
| **Bring** | A shield with good magic block, a light source |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +15 |
| **Goal** | Beat Manus, Father of the Abyss |

## 1. The township

The town is a maze of streets, stairs and broken buildings.

- **Bloated sorcerers** cast **dark magic** and can **steal your humanity** with a grab. Kill them quickly or keep them at a distance.
- **Bloatheads**, their mindless relatives, swarm and explode with dark energy.
- **Take your time.** Every corner has a few enemies waiting.

## 2. Marvelous Chester

A strange, well-dressed man named **Marvelous Chester** appears in the township. He's not from Oolacile either, and he sells a few useful items. He also seems to know more than he says.

## 3. Bonfires and the path down

Find the **township bonfires** as you go; they're well hidden. The way down into the Chasm is through the lower part of the town.

## 4. The Chasm of the Abyss

The **Chasm** is pitch black. Bring a **light** (a lantern or a glowing helmet), or the paths are nearly impossible to follow. More of the Abyss's creatures wander here.

## 5. Manus, Father of the Abyss

At the bottom is **Manus**, the source of the Abyss. He's huge, fast and throws dark magic in the second half of the fight. Read the [Manus boss guide](manus-father-of-the-abyss-boss-guide.html). After the fight, **Dusk** is safe, and Oolacile's story comes to an end.

## Tips

- **Magic defense matters** against the bloated sorcerers and Manus. A shield that blocks magic well is worth carrying.
- **Bring a light** for the Chasm.
- **If you saved Sif's life** from the Abyss earlier in the DLC, you may get help in the final fight.
