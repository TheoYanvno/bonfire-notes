The Sorcerer turns Dark Souls into a game of patience and distance. Soul Arrows hit from far away and do big damage to armored enemies. The trade-off: you are fragile, and a few bad rolls can end a run.

> **In one line:** keep your distance, cast, and never let them corner you.

## Starting class

Pick the **Sorcerer**. You start with a Sorcerer's Catalyst (it casts your spells), the **Soul Arrow** spell, a dagger and a small shield. Your Intelligence and Attunement start high.

## Where to spend your level-up points

| Stat | Early game (to the Taurus Demon) | Mid game (to the Gaping Dragon) | Late game | Final goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intelligence (spell damage) | 20 | 30 | 40 | 40–45 |
| Attunement (spell slots) | starting value | 14 | 16–19 | 19–23 |
| Vitality (health) | 14 | 20 | 30 | 35–40 |
| Endurance (stamina) | 12 | 18 | 25 | 30 |
| Strength, Dexterity | only what your backup weapon needs | | | |
| Faith, Resistance | leave them | leave them | leave them | leave them |

**Why this order:**

- **Intelligence is your damage.** It improves a lot up to 40, then slowly after that.
- **Attunement gives spell slots** only at 10, 12, 14, 16, 19, 23, 28, 34, 41 and 50, so jump between those values.
- **Don't starve Vitality.** A sorcerer with 10 Vitality dies to two arrows. Raise it steadily alongside Intelligence.

## Spells and teachers

| Spell | What it does |
|---|---|
| Soul Arrow | Your starting attack. Fast and cheap. |
| Great Soul Arrow, Heavy Soul Arrow | Stronger versions. Heavy hits harder; Great has more casts. |
| Magic Weapon | Turns your melee weapon into a magic weapon for a while. Great for enemies that resist spells. |
| Soul Spear (later) | A powerful piercing beam. |

- **Griggs of Vinheim:** locked in a house in the Lower Undead Burg. The door needs the **Residence Key**, sold by the merchant on the Undead Burg rooftops. Free him and he moves to Firelink Shrine, where he sells early sorceries.
- **Big Hat Logan:** Griggs' teacher, trapped in a cage in Sen's Fortress. Free him for stronger spells.

## Weapons, armor and shield

| Slot | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Catalyst | Sorcerer's Catalyst early; a stronger catalyst later | It sets how hard your spells hit |
| Backup weapon | Dagger, then any light sword | For enemies that resist magic and for saving spell casts |
| Shield | Small shield or none | Mostly for blocking arrows |
| Armor | Light pieces | Keep the fast roll (under 25% equip load) |

## How to play it

1. **Pull enemies one at a time.** Shoot from far away and let them come to you.
2. **Use corners and doorways** so enemies have to approach in a line.
3. **Keep Magic Weapon ready** for bosses and enemies that resist magic.
4. **Mind your casts.** Each spell has a limited number of uses until you rest at a bonfire, just like Estus.

Bell Gargoyles are a good test for a sorcerer: see the [Bell Gargoyles guide](bell-gargoyles-boss-guide.html).
