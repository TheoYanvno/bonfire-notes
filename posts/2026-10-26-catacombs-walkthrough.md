**The Catacombs** start in the graveyard behind Firelink Shrine and go down into a maze of tombs. It's the first half of the road to **Gravelord Nito**, one of the four Lord Souls. The trick to the whole area: **the skeletons come back to life until you deal with whoever is raising them.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Biggest danger** | Skeletons that reassemble, and wheel skeletons in tight halls |
| **Bring** | A **Divine** weapon if you have one, or a blunt weapon |
| **Recommended** | After the Lordvessel (it can be done earlier, but it's hard) |
| **Goal** | Beat Pinwheel and reach the Tomb of the Giants |

## 1. Why the skeletons get back up

After you knock a skeleton down it lies in a pile of bones, then pulls itself back together. Two ways to stop it:

1. **Kill the necromancers.** These robed casters hide in the halls and keep the skeletons going. Once they're dead, skeletons stay down.
2. **Use a Divine weapon.** Andre can upgrade a weapon down the Divine path with the **Divine Ember** from Darkroot Garden. Skeletons killed with it stay dead.

**Blunt weapons** like maces and clubs also smash skeletons faster.

## 2. The first descent

The entrance is in the **Firelink Shrine graveyard**, past the skeletons you may have seen early in the game. The halls go down in levels connected by stairs and drops.

- **Lure skeletons out** one at a time; they swarm in narrow halls.
- **Watch for traps**: some floors and walls have spikes.

## 3. Wheel skeletons

Skeletons riding spiked wheels roll at you along straight halls and bridges.

- **Get to the side** of their path, or stand behind a pillar.
- **Hit them as they turn around.**
- **Don't fight them on a bridge** where one bump knocks you off.

## 4. Vamos the blacksmith

Deep in the Catacombs, a skeleton blacksmith named **Vamos** works at his forge. He upgrades weapons down the **fire** paths. He's peaceful unless you attack him.

## 5. Bonfire and bridge

There's a bonfire partway down, next to a long bridge with a lever. Lower the bridge, then cross carefully.

## 6. Pinwheel

At the bottom is the fog gate of **Pinwheel**, a strange masked necromancer who makes copies of himself. He's one of the easiest bosses in the game. Read the [Pinwheel boss guide](pinwheel-boss-guide.html). He drops the **Rite of Kindling**, which lets you kindle bonfires to hold more Estus.

## 7. Onward

Past Pinwheel, the way continues down into the **Tomb of the Giants** ([Part 14](tomb-of-the-giants-walkthrough.html)).

## Tips

- **Necromancers first, skeletons second.**
- **A Divine weapon makes this area easy**, and it's worth getting even if you never use it elsewhere.
- **Kindle your bonfire** with the Rite of Kindling before the Tomb. You'll want the extra Estus.
