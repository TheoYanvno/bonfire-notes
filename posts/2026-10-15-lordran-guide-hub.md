## Three rules that fix most problems

1. **Level Vitality and Endurance.** Health and stamina save more runs than damage.
2. **Upgrade one weapon.** Andre in the Undead Parish turns a good weapon into a great one.
3. **If an area feels impossible, you may be on the wrong path.** Turn around and explore somewhere else.
