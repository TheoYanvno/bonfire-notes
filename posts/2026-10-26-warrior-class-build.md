The **Warrior** is the most flexible melee start. Its Strength and Dexterity are both 13, so it can pick up nearly any sword, axe or spear you find, and its Heater Shield blocks all physical damage. This guide turns it into a **quality build**: Strength and Dexterity raised together.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting level** | 4 |
| **Stats** | Vitality 11 · Attunement 8 · Endurance 12 · Strength 13 · Dexterity 13 · Resistance 11 · Intelligence 9 · Faith 9 |
| **Weapon** | Longsword |
| **Shield** | Heater Shield (blocks 100% of physical damage) |
| **Armor** | Standard Helm and the Hard Leather set |
| **Plays like** | Sword and board: block, punish, roll when you have to |

> **In one line:** a well-rounded fighter who never has to pass up a good weapon because of its stat requirements.

## What the Warrior is good at

- **No wasted points.** Attunement 8, Intelligence 9 and Faith 9 are the stats this build never uses, and they start low.
- **A real shield from minute one.** Many classes start with a shield that lets some damage through. The Heater Shield doesn't.
- **Weapon freedom.** Quality builds get the most out of weapons that scale with both Strength and Dexterity, like the Claymore and the Black Knight Sword.

## What to level up

| Stat | Early (to the Taurus Demon) | Mid (to the Gaping Dragon) | Late (Anor Londo) | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vitality | 16 | 25 | 30 | 40 |
| Endurance | 16 | 25 | 30 | 40 |
| Strength | 16 | 20 | 27 | 40 |
| Dexterity | 14 | 18 | 27 | 40 |
| Everything else | leave it | leave it | leave it | leave it |

- **Strength 16 first** so you can use the Claymore you find in the Undead Burg.
- **Vitality and Endurance keep you alive.** Health means fewer deaths; stamina means more blocks and swings before you run dry.
- **40 is the soft cap** for Strength and Dexterity: past it, each point adds much less damage.

## Weapons

| Weapon | Where | Why use it |
|---|---|---|
| Longsword | Starting gear (also sold by Andre) | Fast, reliable, upgrades well. Good through the Undead Parish. |
| Claymore | Undead Burg, on the bridge the red drake guards | Wide sweeps and a lunging thrust. A great all-rounder. |
| Balder Side Sword | Dropped by the Balder Knights in the Undead Parish | Quick thrusts that pierce shields; strong with Dexterity. |
| Black Knight Sword | Rare drop from Black Knights | One of the best quality weapons in the game, if you're lucky. |

Pick one main weapon and upgrade it with **Andre** in the Undead Parish. A +10 weapon you know well beats a stronger one at +0.

## Armor, shield and rings

| Slot | Early | Later |
|---|---|---|
| Armor | Hard Leather set | Elite Knight set (Darkroot Garden) |
| Shield | Heater Shield | Grass Crest Shield (Darkroot Basin, guarded by a Black Knight): faster stamina recovery |
| Rings | Havel's Ring (Havel the Rock, in the Undead Burg watchtower) | Wolf Ring (Darkroot Garden) for poise |

**Watch your equip load.** Stay under 50% so you keep a normal roll instead of a slow "fat roll".

## How to play it

1. **Walk with your shield up** in new areas.
2. **Attack after their swing, not during it.** Two hits, then guard again.
3. **Two-hand your weapon** (press Y / Triangle) against bosses that don't need blocking. Two-handing counts your Strength as 1.5×.
4. **Kick shield users.** A kick breaks their guard and gives you a free hit.
