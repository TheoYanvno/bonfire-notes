**Crossbreed Priscilla** is half dragon, half something else, and was locked away in the Painted World. She's **completely optional** and won't attack unless you do. If you choose to fight, the challenge is that **she turns invisible** after taking damage.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Painted World of Ariamis, at the end of the castle |
| **Reward** | **Soul of Priscilla**, and **Priscilla's Dagger** if you cut her tail |
| **Recommended** | After Ornstein and Smough, weapon at +10, 30 Vitality |

## Her attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Scythe sweeps | Long, wide swings | Roll toward her and past the blade |
| Bleed | Every scythe hit builds up bleeding | Back off and heal before the meter fills |
| Invisibility | She vanishes after being hit | Watch for her **footprints in the snow** |
| Tail swipe | A sweep with her long tail | Don't stand right behind her for long |

## Strategy

1. **Open with your strongest hit** while she's still visible and peaceful. A backstab works.
2. **Cut her tail early.** Hit it while she's busy with her scythe; it gives Priscilla's Dagger, and you get the chance only once.
3. **When she disappears, stop and watch the snow.** Her footprints show where she is.
4. **Bleed is the real threat.** Don't trade hits for long.
5. **Or don't fight her at all.** Talk to her and leave. Nobody will judge you.

## After the fight

Jump off the edge where you entered the Painted World to return to Anor Londo. Back to the [guides hub](lordran-guide-hub.html).
