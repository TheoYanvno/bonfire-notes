**Ceaseless Discharge** is a giant fire demon, one of the children of the Witch of Izalith. He lives in the lava of the Demon Ruins and doesn't notice you until you pick up the **Gold-Hemmed Black set**, the clothes of his sister. Then he rises, angry. **There are two ways to beat him: on his arms, or with the edge.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Demon Ruins, the long path above the lava |
| **Trigger** | Picking up the Gold-Hemmed Black set on the ledge |
| **Resists** | Fire |
| **Reward** | **Souls** and a shortcut through the lava |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +10, 30 Vitality, fire resistance |

## His attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Arm slam | Brings his flaming arm down on the path | Roll away from where it's falling, toward the cliff wall |
| Sweep | Drags his arm along the path | Get to the wall side; the arm doesn't reach as far there |
| Fire ground | His slams leave burning patches | Don't stand in them |

## The well-known way: let him fall

1. **Pick up the set**, then **run back the way you came**, all the way to the start of the path.
2. **Keep going past the point where the ledge ends.** He follows you, reaching over the cliff.
3. If you're far enough back, **he lunges for you, misses, and falls** into the lava below. Fight over.

## The fair way: hit his arms

1. **Stay close to the cliff wall.**
2. **After each arm slam, his arm rests on the ledge.** Hit it as many times as you can before he pulls it back.
3. **Watch the burning ground** and move before it hurts you.

## After the fight

The lava drains in part of the ruins, opening the way forward. Back to the [guides hub](lordran-guide-hub.html).
