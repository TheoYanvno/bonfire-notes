**Chaos Witch Quelaag** waits in her lair past the Blighttown swamp. The top half of her is a woman with a flaming sword. The bottom half is a giant spider that spits lava. She hits hard, but she gives clear warnings before her worst attacks. **Learn the warnings and stay close to her legs.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Quelaag's Domain, at the far side of the Blighttown swamp |
| **Resists** | Fire. Don't use pyromancies or fire weapons on her |
| **Reward** | **Soul of Quelaag** and souls; the second Bell of Awakening is just up the stairs |
| **Help** | Maneater Mildred can be summoned outside the fog if you didn't kill her |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +5 to +7, 25 Vitality, a shield with good fire protection |

## Her attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Sword swipes | Quick slashes with her flaming sword | Roll toward her, past the blade, or block with a fire-resistant shield |
| Lava spit | The spider lowers its head and spits lava forward | Get to her side; the lava pools stay on the floor for a while |
| Fire blast | She raises both arms and glows | **Run away immediately.** A burst of fire hits everything close to her |
| Leg stomps | Rears up and stamps down | Roll away when her front legs lift |

## Strategy

1. **Get to her side right away.** Most of her attacks go forward.
2. **Attack her legs**, two or three hits at a time, then get your guard back up.
3. **When she raises her arms and glows, run.** This fire blast is what kills most players. Come back as soon as it ends.
4. **Watch the floor.** Lava pools stay for several seconds. Don't roll into them.
5. **Heal when she's spitting lava** in another direction, not while she's swinging.

## Summoning help

If Maneater Mildred is still around, her summon sign is near the fog gate. Two targets split Quelaag's attention and give you free hits on her legs.

## After the fight

Take the stairs at the back of the room up to the bell tower and ring the **second Bell of Awakening**. With both bells rung, the gate to Sen's Fortress opens. That's six bosses down. Back to the [guides hub](lordran-guide-hub.html).
