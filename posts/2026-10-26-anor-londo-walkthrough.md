**Anor Londo** is the city of the gods, bathed in golden light. It's beautiful and quiet, and guarded by **Silver Knights**, the gods' own soldiers. Two infamous sections live here: a narrow walk along the cathedral's buttresses, and two archers on a ledge. After that comes the most famous boss fight in the game.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Biggest danger** | Silver Knights, the buttress walk, and the two archers |
| **Bring** | Your best shield, and arrows if you have them |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +10, 30 Vitality |
| **Goal** | Beat Ornstein and Smough and receive the Lordvessel |

## 1. The first bonfire

You arrive on a rooftop with a bonfire. The **Darkmoon Knightess** sits near the bonfire, pretending to be a Fire Keeper. She'll matter later; don't attack her.

## 2. The spinning tower

Ahead is a tower with a lever. Pulling it **rotates the bridge** at the tower's base. The order:

1. Ride the elevator down and cross the first way.
2. Use the lever to turn the tower so the bridge lines up with the next path.

There's also a lever that turns it back, so you can always return to the bonfire.

## 3. The flying buttresses

Inside a large hall, you climb out onto the **rafters and the flying buttresses** (the arched supports on the outside of the cathedral). They're narrow, and **Silver Knights** wait at the ends.

- **Walk, don't roll.**
- **Fight on the wide sections.** Let the knight come to you.
- **Take off heavy armor** if you keep slipping; nothing up here needs it more than you need your footing.

## 4. The archers on the ledge

Past the buttresses, a long, narrow ledge climbs up the side of the building, and **two Silver Knight archers** shoot huge arrows that knock you off.

- **Walk up the ledge with your shield raised**, angled so the arrows hit it.
- **Rush the closer archer.** On the ledge the arrows are your enemy, not the archer.
- **Then roll to the other archer and kill it.** It drops a **Dragonslayer Greatbow** on some runs.

## 5. The main city and the Giant Blacksmith

Below the ledge are the city's streets and courtyards. Look for:

- **The Giant Blacksmith**, a huge, gentle smith who can upgrade weapons and sells special arrows.
- **Painting Guardians**, nimble swordsmen in robes. They drop a fast curved sword.
- **A second bonfire**, near the cathedral. Rest there before the boss.

## 6. The cathedral

Inside the cathedral, giant knights with huge shields guard the halls. They're slow but block well; get behind them. At the end is the fog gate of **Ornstein and Smough**. Read the [Ornstein and Smough guide](ornstein-and-smough.html). **Solaire** can be summoned near the fog gate if you've kept talking to him.

## 7. The Lordvessel

After the fight, an elevator takes you up to **Gwynevere, Princess of Sunlight**. She gives you the **Lordvessel**. Take it to the great snake **Frampt** at Firelink Shrine, and from then on you can **warp between bonfires**.

## Optional

- **The Painted World:** a room in the city holds a huge painting. With the Peculiar Doll from the Undead Asylum you can enter it. See [Part 10](painted-world-walkthrough.html).
- **Dark Sun Gwyndolin:** a hidden god waits in a tomb beneath the city. See the [Gwyndolin boss guide](dark-sun-gwyndolin-boss-guide.html).

## Tips

- **Silver Knights block well.** Wait for their swing to end, then hit them twice.
- **Use the two bonfires** as checkpoints: the first one for the buttresses and archers, the second one for the boss.
