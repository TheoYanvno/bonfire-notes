The **Wanderer** is a swordsman who lives by speed. It starts with Dexterity 14 and a **Scimitar**, a curved sword with quick, chaining slashes. This guide builds a **Dexterity duelist**: light armor, fast weapons and rolling through attacks instead of blocking them.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Starting level** | 3 |
| **Stats** | Vitality 10 · Attunement 11 · Endurance 10 · Strength 10 · Dexterity 14 · Resistance 12 · Intelligence 11 · Faith 8 |
| **Weapon** | Scimitar |
| **Shield** | Leather Shield |
| **Armor** | The Wanderer set (hood, coat, manchette, boots) |
| **Plays like** | Dodge, slash two or three times, dodge again |

> **In one line:** less armor, more speed. Learn the roll and this build feels amazing.

## What the Wanderer is good at

- **Fast attacks** that interrupt enemies before they can swing.
- **Light gear** keeps you in the fast-roll range for the whole game.
- **The downside:** Vitality 10 means few early mistakes are allowed. Raise it first.

## What to level up

| Stat | Early (to the Taurus Demon) | Mid (to the Gaping Dragon) | Late (Anor Londo) | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vitality | 18 | 25 | 30 | 40 |
| Endurance | 15 | 22 | 30 | 35 |
| Dexterity | 16 | 22 | 30 | 40 |
| Strength | 12 | 14 | 16 | 16 |
| Everything else | leave it | leave it | leave it | leave it |

- **Vitality before damage.** A fast build still dies in two hits at Vitality 10.
- **Dexterity to 40** for the most damage from curved swords, katanas and thrusting swords.
- **A little Strength** (12 to 16) lets you use better shields and a few weapons that need it.

## Weapons

| Weapon | Where | Why use it |
|---|---|---|
| Scimitar | Starting gear | Fast chains of slashes. Upgrade it early; it stays good for a long time. |
| Balder Side Sword | Dropped by the Balder Knights in the Undead Parish | Thrusts that go through shields. Great with Dexterity. |
| Painting Guardian Sword | Dropped by the Painting Guardians in Anor Londo | A very fast curved sword with long chains. |
| Silver Knight Straight Sword | Dropped by Silver Knights in Anor Londo | A strong, clean Dexterity sword for the late game. |

Upgrade one weapon at a time with **Andre**. Later in the game a **Sharp** upgrade (it needs an ember you find along the way) raises Dexterity scaling even further.

## Armor, shield and rings

| Slot | Early | Later |
|---|---|---|
| Armor | Wanderer set | Light pieces you like; stay under 25% equip load for the fastest roll |
| Shield | Leather Shield (good for parrying) | Grass Crest Shield (Darkroot Basin) |
| Rings | Hornet Ring (behind Artorias's grave, past Sif's arena): bigger critical hits | Dark Wood Grain Ring (dropped by Shiva of the East's bodyguard) changes your roll into a fast flip |

## How to play it

1. **Learn to roll through attacks**, not away from them. Rolling toward and past an enemy puts you behind it.
2. **Backstab and parry.** Light weapons make both easy, and criticals with the Hornet Ring hit very hard.
3. **Two or three hits, then out.** Greed kills fast builds.
4. **Watch your equip load.** Under 25% is a fast roll; going over makes this build much harder.
