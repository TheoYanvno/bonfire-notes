Remember the **Northern Undead Asylum**, where the game started? You can go back, and something new is waiting: the **Stray Demon**, a bigger, meaner version of the Asylum Demon, hidden beneath the floor of the first boss's arena. The trip is worth it for the **Peculiar Doll** (the key to the Painted World) and a **Titanite Slab**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **How to get back** | At Firelink Shrine, climb to the ledge near the big tree and curl up. A crow carries you to the Asylum. |
| **Where** | Under the Asylum Demon's arena; the floor gives way |
| **Resists** | Fire. Don't bother with pyromancies |
| **Reward** | **Titanite Slab** and souls |
| **Recommended** | After Anor Londo, weapon at +10, 30 Vitality |

## Back in the Asylum

The Asylum has a few new enemies and some new items. Look around your old cell area for the **Peculiar Doll**. Then walk into the Asylum Demon's old arena. **The floor collapses** and you fall into the Stray Demon's pit.

## Its attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Hammer smashes | Heavy overhead and side swings | Stay behind it |
| Magic explosion | It raises its hammer and slams down with a huge blue blast | **Run away immediately**; this kills most players |
| Butt slam | Jumps and lands on its backside | Get out from under it |
| Charge | Rushes forward | Roll sideways |

## Strategy

1. **Get behind it right away** and stay there.
2. **When it raises the hammer high, run.** The magic blast covers a wide area.
3. **Hit the tail and legs**, two or three hits, then step back.
4. **Magic-blocking shields help** against the explosion.
5. **Heal when it's slow to turn**, never in front of it.

## After the fight

Collect the **Titanite Slab**, perfect for a +15 weapon. With the Peculiar Doll you can now enter the Painted World in Anor Londo ([Part 10](painted-world-walkthrough.html)). Back to the [guides hub](lordran-guide-hub.html).
