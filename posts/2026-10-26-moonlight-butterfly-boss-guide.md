The **Moonlight Butterfly** is a huge, glowing moth that lives on a stone bridge at the top of a tower in Darkroot Garden. Most of the fight it floats beside the bridge, out of reach, firing magic. **Every so often it lands on the bridge to rest. That's your moment.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Darkroot Garden, through the fog gate at the tower |
| **Resists** | Magic. Use physical damage, fire or arrows |
| **Reward** | **Soul of the Moonlight Butterfly**, and the tower behind it with the **Divine Ember** |
| **Help** | **Witch Beatrice** can be summoned; her sign is under the stairs before the fog gate |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +5, 20 Vitality, a bow or fire if you have them |

## Its attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Magic orbs | It glows and fires a spray of homing blue orbs | Run sideways along the bridge; they curve but can be outrun |
| Magic beam | A slow-charging beam aimed down the bridge | Step to one side; it fires in a straight line |
| Swoop | It moves to the other side of the bridge | Use the time to heal |

## Strategy

1. **Don't chase it.** It's over the drop, and you'll fall.
2. **Keep moving sideways** along the bridge while it attacks, so the orbs miss.
3. **When it lands on the bridge, sprint to it** and hit it until it flies off. Two-handing your weapon gives the most damage in that window.
4. **Use ranged attacks in between.** Arrows, firebombs and pyromancies all hit it while it hovers. Sorceries do little; it resists magic.
5. **Summon Beatrice.** Her sorceries keep it busy and draw its attacks.

## After the fight

Cross the bridge into the tower and climb to the top for the **Divine Ember**. Give it to Andre to upgrade weapons down the Divine path, which you'll want for the [Catacombs](catacombs-walkthrough.html). Back to the [guides hub](lordran-guide-hub.html).
