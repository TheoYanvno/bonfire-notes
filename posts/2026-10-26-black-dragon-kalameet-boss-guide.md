**Black Dragon Kalameet** is the last of the great dragons, and he's optional. You can't fight him at first: he flies over the valley. You need the help of **Hawkeye Gough**, a giant archer, to shoot him down. **Once he lands, stay out of his front.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Royal Wood, the valley below the Oolacile area |
| **Unlock** | Get the **Crest Key** and talk to **Hawkeye Gough** in his tower, after you have seen Kalameet fly by |
| **Watch out for** | **Mark of Calamity**: he can put a curse on you that **doubles the damage you take** for a while |
| **Reward** | Souls, and the **Obsidian Greatsword** if you cut his tail |
| **Recommended** | Weapon at +12 to +15, 40 Vitality |

## His attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Fire breath | A long sweep of fire | Get to his side or behind him |
| Mark of Calamity | Glows and a mark lands on you | Back off and play safe until it fades |
| Charge | Rushes across the arena | Roll sideways |
| Tail swipe | Spins his tail | Hit the tail, then step back |
| Bite and claws | Fast attacks in front | Don't stand in front |

## Strategy

1. **Talk to Hawkeye Gough** first. Without him Kalameet can't be fought.
2. **Stand at his side or back legs.** His most dangerous attacks go forward.
3. **When you're marked, stop being greedy.** Every hit hurts twice as much.
4. **Cut his tail** for the Obsidian Greatsword.
5. **Bring lots of Estus.** This is one of the hardest fights in the game.
