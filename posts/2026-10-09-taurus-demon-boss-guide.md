The Taurus Demon is the first boss that can feel unfair. It is fast for its size, the wall you fight on is narrow, and two archers shoot at you from behind. But it has a famous weakness, and once you know it, the fight is short.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The long stone wall in the Undead Burg |
| **Weak to** | Plunging attacks, fire (firebombs) |
| **Reward** | Souls, and the way forward to the red dragon bridge |
| **Recommended** | A weapon at +3 or more, a few firebombs, about 15 Vitality |

## Before the fog

**Kill the two hollow archers** on the tower at the start of the wall, or they will pepper you with arrows during the fight. Approach them carefully; one can knock you off the tower.

## The trick: the ladder

When you walk through the fog, the demon leaps onto the far end of the wall and charges.

1. **Turn around and run back** to the tower you came from.
2. **Climb the ladder** inside the tower to the top.
3. Wait for the demon to walk underneath you, then **drop down and press attack** for a plunging attack.
4. If it doesn't die, run back toward the ladder and climb again. It often follows you underneath for another plunge.

Two or three plunges plus some hits usually finish it.

## Its attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Overhead slam | Raises its axe high, slams down | Roll to the side or between its legs |
| Wide swing | Big horizontal swing | Roll through it toward its body |
| Leap | Jumps into the air and lands on you | Run sideways or forward under it |
| Grab-like swipe | Close-range sweep | Stay behind, not in front |

## If you fight on the wall

- **Stand between or behind its legs.** Most of its attacks hit the space in front of it.
- **Throw firebombs** from a distance if you have them. They do strong damage early in the game.
- **Never stand with your back to the edge.** Its swings can knock you off the wall.
- **Pyromancers:** a few fireballs from the far end of the wall work well.

## After the fight

Continue through the next tower toward the bridge and meet Solaire. Next boss: [Bell Gargoyles](bell-gargoyles-boss-guide.html).
