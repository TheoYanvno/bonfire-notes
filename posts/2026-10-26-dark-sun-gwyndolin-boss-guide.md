**Dark Sun Gwyndolin** is the youngest child of Gwyn and the keeper of Anor Londo's secrets. He's optional and fragile, but he fights from far away with magic, at the end of a long hallway that **stretches as you run down it.** The answer: **use the pillars, and never stop moving forward.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Anor Londo, in the **Darkmoon Tomb** |
| **Resists** | Magic |
| **Reward** | **Soul of Gwyndolin** |
| **Warning** | Fighting him has consequences for the Darkmoon covenant and for what you've seen in Anor Londo |
| **Recommended** | After Ornstein and Smough, weapon at +10, 30 Vitality |

## His attacks

| Attack | What it looks like | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Magic arrows | A spread of glowing arrows from his bow | Step behind a pillar |
| Soul spears | Large magic spears that home in | Break line of sight, or roll at the last moment |
| Orbs | Slow magic balls | Easy to outrun; don't let them corner you |
| Teleport | He vanishes and reappears further down the hall | Keep heading toward him |

## Strategy

1. **Move from pillar to pillar.** Wait behind one for a volley to end, then sprint to the next.
2. **The hallway is an illusion** that grows when you're slow. Steady progress shrinks it.
3. **When you reach him, hit him hard.** He has little health and can't take many hits.
4. **Shields with high magic block** help a lot.
5. **Archers and casters** can damage him from range, but closing in is usually faster.

Read the soul's description; it explains some of Anor Londo's secrets. Back to the [guides hub](lordran-guide-hub.html).
