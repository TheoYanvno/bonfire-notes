Build Guide: The Dexterity Swordsman

If the Strength Knight is a wall, the Dexterity Swordsman is a blade. You wear lighter armor, roll out of danger instead of blocking, and land fast hits that add up quickly. It is harder to learn than a shield build, but very rewarding.

In one line: dodge through the attack, punish with two or three quick hits, dodge out.

Starting class

The Wanderer starts with high Dexterity and a scimitar, and the Thief starts with good Dexterity and a dagger built for backstabs. Either works. Pick the Knight if you want a safer start and plan to switch to Dexterity weapons later.

Where to spend your level-up points

Stat Early game (to the Taurus Demon) Mid game (to the Gaping Dragon) Late game Final goal
Vitality (health) 15 22 30 40
Endurance (stamina, equip load) 15 22 30 40
Dexterity (damage with fast weapons) 14 24 34 40
Strength 14 14 16 16
Attunement, Intelligence, Faith, Resistance leave them leave them leave them leave them

Why this order:

  • Strength 14 and Dexterity 14 open up most katanas and straight swords. After that, Strength only needs to grow for heavier armor or a specific weapon.
  • Dexterity scales your damage with fast weapons. Like Strength, it gives much less after 40.
  • Endurance is extra important here: every roll and every swing costs stamina, and you don’t have a big shield to fall back on.
  • Dexterity also slightly speeds up casting, which matters only if you add spells later.

Weapons

Weapon Where Why use it
Scimitar Wanderer starting gear Very fast curved sword. Fine for the first areas.
Uchigatana Sold by the merchant on the rooftops of the Undead Burg A katana that causes bleeding. Long reach and fast swings. Save up your souls for it.
Balder Side Sword Rare drop from Balder Knights in the Undead Parish A thrusting sword that can hit through shields.

Bleeding builds up with each hit. When the bar fills, the enemy takes a large chunk of damage at once. It is perfect for fast weapons that land many hits.

Upgrades: take your weapon to Andre in the Undead Parish. Keep it on the normal upgrade path until you understand the elemental paths; normal weapons keep their Dexterity scaling.

Armor and shield

  • Armor: stay light. Your goal is the fast roll, which needs your equip load under 25%. Mix pieces from different sets until you get there. Leather and light chain armor work well early.
  • Shield: a light shield on your back, mainly to block arrows. Many Dexterity players use a small shield like a Buckler for parrying (knocking an attack aside, then landing a big riposte).

Rings

  • Hornet Ring: boosts critical hits, which means backstabs and ripostes. On a fast character that already loves backstabs, it is a big damage boost.
  • Havel’s Ring if you want to wear slightly heavier armor and still keep a fast roll.

How to play it

  1. Roll toward the enemy, not away. Rolling through an attack puts you behind them for a free hit or a backstab.
  2. Count your stamina. Always keep enough for one roll after attacking.
  3. Backstab: walk behind an enemy that isn’t facing you and press attack for a critical hit.
  4. Two-hand your weapon for extra damage when you’re comfortable dodging.
