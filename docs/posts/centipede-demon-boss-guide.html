Boss Guide 18: Centipede Demon

guidebosses

In this post
  1. Its attacks
  2. Strategy
  3. After the fight

The Centipede Demon is a mass of centipede-like arms with a mouth in the middle, and it lives in a lava lake. You fight it on a few islands of solid ground. The danger is as much the lava as the demon. Stay on solid ground, and cut off its arms.
Where Demon Ruins, by the lava lake near the bottom
Resists Fire
Reward Orange Charred Ring (greatly reduces lava damage) and souls
Recommended Weapon at +10 to +12, 30 Vitality

Its attacks

Attack What it looks like How to avoid it
Arm swipes Its long arms slam and sweep Roll away from the arm, not into the lava
Fire spit Fireballs from the mouth Sidestep
Leap Jumps onto your island Roll out of the landing spot
Grab Grabs you with its arms Keep moving; don’t stand right in front of it

Strategy

  1. Never roll toward the lava. Know where the edges of your island are.
  2. Hit the arms. Each arm can be cut off, which makes it weaker.
  3. Let it come to you. Don’t chase it across the lava.
  4. Use ranged attacks (arrows) if it stays out in the lava.

After the fight

Put on the Orange Charred Ring. You’ll need it for the lava fields of Lost Izalith (Part 16). Back to the guides hub.
