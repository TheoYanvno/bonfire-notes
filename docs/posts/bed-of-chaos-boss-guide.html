Boss Guide 19: Bed of Chaos

guidebosses

In this post
  1. Its attacks
  2. Strategy
  3. After the fight

The Bed of Chaos is what’s left of the Witch of Izalith: a giant tree of roots and flame with something small at its heart. It’s more puzzle than fight. Break the two glowing roots, then reach the core. Even better: the roots stay broken if you die.
Where Lost Izalith, the great root chamber
Reward A Lord Soul (for the Lordvessel)
Recommended Weapon at +10, 30 Vitality

Its attacks

Attack What it looks like How to avoid it
Branch sweeps Huge arms sweep across the floor Run, or roll through as they pass
Fire Fire bursts and streams from the roots Keep moving
Falling floor Parts of the floor collapse into pits Watch where you step; the holes open fast

Strategy

  1. Pick a side, left or right. Each side has a glowing root protected by branches.
  2. Run along that side to the root. The floor will break up as you go; keep to solid ground.
  3. Break the root. Once it’s broken, it stays broken even if you die.
  4. Do the same on the other side.
  5. The core: with both roots broken, the middle opens. Walk out on the branch in the middle and drop down into the center. The core is small and weak. Hit it and it’s over.

After the fight

Place the Lord Soul in the Lordvessel. Back to the guides hub.
