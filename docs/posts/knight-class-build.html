Class Build: Knight

The Knight is the gentlest start in Dark Souls. It begins with the most health of any class (Vitality 14), a full set of heavy armor and the Tower Kite Shield. This guide turns it into a Strength tank: heavy armor, a big shield and hits that knock enemies down.
Starting level 5
Stats Vitality 14 · Attunement 10 · Endurance 10 · Strength 11 · Dexterity 11 · Resistance 10 · Intelligence 9 · Faith 11
Weapon Broadsword
Shield Tower Kite Shield
Armor The Knight set (helm, armor, gauntlets, leggings)
Plays like Block, wait for the opening, hit hard once, block again

In one line: hard to kill, easy to learn. If you are new to the series, start here.

What the Knight is good at

  • Mistakes cost less. More health and real armor mean you survive the hits you didn’t see coming.
  • Starting armor lasts. The Knight set is still decent hours into the game.
  • The downside: Endurance 10 is low, so blocking and swinging drain your stamina fast at first. Raise it early.

What to level up

Stat Early (to the Taurus Demon) Mid (to the Gaping Dragon) Late (Anor Londo) Goal
Vitality 18 25 30 40
Endurance 18 25 32 40
Strength 16 24 32 40
Dexterity leave it leave it 12–14 14
Everything else leave it leave it leave it leave it
  • Endurance first to fix the Knight’s weak spot. It raises both stamina and how much you can carry.
  • Strength 16 opens the Claymore. Strength 24 lets you one-hand the Zweihander, but two-handing counts your Strength as 1.5×, so 16 is enough to swing it with both hands.
  • Skip Resistance. Armor protects you better than those points would.

Weapons

Weapon Where Why use it
Broadsword Starting gear Simple and dependable. Fine for the first areas.
Claymore Undead Burg, on the bridge the red drake guards Big sweeps that hit groups. Strength 16.
Zweihander Firelink Shrine graveyard (watch for skeletons) Huge damage and stagger. Strength 24, or two-hand it at 16.
Black Knight Greataxe or Sword Rare drops from Black Knights End-game damage for a Strength build.

Upgrades: Andre in the Undead Parish uses Titanite Shards up to +5, then Large Titanite Shards, Titanite Chunks and a Titanite Slab for +15. Commit to one weapon.

Armor, shield and rings

Slot Early Later
Armor Knight set Elite Knight set (Darkroot Garden) or the Stone Knight set (a chest in the woods behind the Crest of Artorias door)
Shield Tower Kite Shield Grass Crest Shield (Darkroot Basin): faster stamina recovery
Rings Havel’s Ring: +50% equip load Wolf Ring (Darkroot Garden): more poise, so small hits don’t stagger you

Keep your equip load under 50% to roll normally. Havel’s Ring is what lets a Knight wear heavy armor and still dodge.

How to play it

  1. Shield up while you walk. Most ambushes bounce off.
  2. Never empty your stamina bar. Leave a little for a block or a roll.
  3. Two-hand big weapons against bosses you plan to dodge.
  4. Kick shielded enemies to open them up.
