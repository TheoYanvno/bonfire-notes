Class Build: Cleric

The Cleric is a fighter-healer. It starts with Faith 14, a Mace that hits hard against armored enemies, a talisman and the Heal miracle. This guide builds a Faith knight: a sturdy melee fighter who heals between fights and later throws lightning spears.
Starting level 2
Stats Vitality 11 · Attunement 11 · Endurance 9 · Strength 12 · Dexterity 8 · Resistance 11 · Intelligence 8 · Faith 14
Weapon Mace
Shield East-West Shield
Talisman Canvas Talisman (hold it to cast miracles)
Miracle Heal
Armor Holy Robe, Traveling Gloves and Holy Trousers
Plays like Strength melee, with heals that save Estus

In one line: a melee build with a second health bar in its pocket.

What the Cleric is good at

  • Healing without Estus. Heal restores a big chunk of health; between fights it saves your flasks for the boss.
  • Blunt damage. Maces and clubs are strong against armored knights.
  • Lightning later on. Faith powers the lightning miracles, which are very strong against dragons and many bosses.
  • The downside: Endurance 9 and Dexterity 8. Raise Endurance early.

What to level up

Stat Early (to the Taurus Demon) Mid (to the Gaping Dragon) Late (Anor Londo) Goal
Vitality 16 25 30 40
Endurance 15 22 30 35
Faith 16 20 25 40
Strength 14 16 20 24
Attunement 11 12 14 16
  • Faith 20 lets you use Lightning Spear, and 25 is what joining the Warriors of Sunlight asks for (helping other players beat bosses lowers that).
  • Attunement 12 to 16 gives 2 to 4 slots, enough for Heal plus a couple of attack miracles.

Miracles and where to get them

Miracle From Why use it
Heal Starting miracle Heals a lot, slowly. Cast it behind a corner.
Seek Guidance, Replenishment, Force Petrus of Thorolund at Firelink Shrine (he also leads the Way of White covenant) Force knocks enemies off ledges and stops arrows.
Lightning Spear Join the Warriors of Sunlight at the Sunlight Altar in the Undead Parish (Solaire, who you meet in the Undead Burg, is a member) A thrown spear of lightning that scales with Faith.

Weapons

Weapon Where Why use it
Mace Starting gear Strong against armor, and upgrades well.
A Divine weapon Get the Divine Ember from the tower in Darkroot Garden and have Andre upgrade a weapon down the Divine path Scales with Faith, and stops skeletons in the Catacombs from getting back up.
Claymore or Zweihander Undead Burg bridge / Firelink graveyard If you’d rather lean on Strength.

Armor, shield and rings

Slot Early Later
Armor Holy set Elite Knight set (Darkroot Garden)
Shield East-West Shield Grass Crest Shield (Darkroot Basin): faster stamina recovery
Rings Havel’s Ring Wolf Ring (Darkroot Garden)

How to play it

  1. Heal between fights, Estus during them. Heal is slow to cast; never use it with an enemy next to you.
  2. Hit armored enemies with the mace. Blunt weapons don’t bounce off armor.
  3. Learn Force for enemies on ledges and to swat arrows out of the air.
