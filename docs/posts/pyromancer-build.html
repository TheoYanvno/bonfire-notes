Build Guide: The Pyromancer

Pyromancy is the quiet secret of Dark Souls. Unlike sorcery or miracles, pyromancy damage does not depend on your stats at all. It depends only on how far you upgrade your Pyromancy Flame. That means a pyromancer can put nearly every level into health and stamina and still hit hard with fire.

In one line: a melee build that also throws fireballs.

Starting class

Pick the Pyromancer. You start with the Pyromancy Flame, the Fireball spell, a Hand Axe and a small shield. It is also the lowest starting level, so you can shape it however you like.

Where to spend your level-up points

Stat Early game (to the Taurus Demon) Mid game (to the Gaping Dragon) Late game Final goal
Vitality (health) 16 25 30 40
Endurance (stamina, equip load) 16 25 30 40
Attunement (spell slots) starting value 12 14–16 16
Strength or Dexterity (your melee weapon) 12 16 20–24 24–30
Intelligence, Faith, Resistance leave them leave them leave them leave them

Why this order:

  • Fire damage comes from the flame, not your stats, so your points go into staying alive.
  • Attunement gives spell slots only at certain values: 10, 12, 14, 16, 19, 23, 28, 34, 41 and 50. A point at 11 or 13 does nothing for slots, so jump straight to the next breakpoint.
  • Pick one melee stat. Strength if you like axes and maces, Dexterity if you like swords. Your fire handles the rest.

Spells

Spell What it does When to use it
Fireball A thrown fireball Your bread and butter. Soften up enemies before they reach you.
Combustion A burst of fire right in front of you Huge close-range damage. Great on bosses that stand still.
Fire Orb, Great Fireball Stronger thrown fire Mid game upgrades once you have more slots.
Poison Mist, Power Within Utility and buffs Fun later, but not needed for a first playthrough.

The flame and the teachers

  • Laurentius: a fellow pyromancer trapped in a barrel in the Depths, guarded by monsters. Rescue him and he waits at Firelink Shrine. He upgrades your flame for souls (+1 to +10) and teaches new pyromancies.
  • Quelana: a later teacher in Blighttown, after you defeat the Chaos Witch Quelaag and fully upgrade your flame. She teaches the strongest fire spells.

Upgrade your flame every time you can afford it. It is your biggest damage boost.

Weapons, armor and shield

Slot Early Later
Weapon Hand Axe (starting gear) Any Strength or Dexterity weapon you like, upgraded at Andre
Shield Small starting shield A 100% physical block shield, so blocking doesn’t hurt
Armor Starting robes Medium armor that keeps you under 50% equip load

Your Pyromancy Flame goes in your left hand, so you swap between flame and shield. Many pyromancers keep the flame in the left hand full time and dodge instead of blocking.

How to play it

  1. Open with a fireball as enemies approach, then finish them with your weapon.
  2. Save Estus Flasks for real trouble. Your spells refill at bonfires, just like Estus.
  3. Fire is weak against some enemies. Many dragons and fire-born demons shrug it off, so carry a decent weapon.

For a boss where a few fireballs make a big difference, see the Taurus Demon guide.
