Walkthrough Part 4: The Undead Parish

The Undead Parish is where Dark Souls starts to open up. It has the game’s first real blacksmith, a shortcut straight back to Firelink Shrine, and the first of the two Bells of Awakening on its roof. You get here by crossing the red drake’s bridge at the end of the Undead Burg.
Biggest danger The Fang Boar in the courtyard, and the Black Knight past the church
Bring Firebombs, and souls to spend on upgrades
Recommended Weapon at +3 or higher, 15+ Vitality
Goal Ring the first Bell of Awakening

First thing: find Andre, the blacksmith. Upgrading your weapon here makes everything else easier.

1. The courtyard and the boar

Past the bridge you come to a courtyard guarded by soldiers and a heavily armored Fang Boar. Its front is nearly unbreakable, so don’t fight it head-on.

  • Lure it into a charge, roll aside, and hit its back legs.
  • Or skip it: run along the left side and up the stairs into the church grounds.
  • Firebombs and fire work well if you’d rather kill it from a distance.

A Halberd and other gear wait around the courtyard. Grab them once the area is quiet.

2. Into the church

Inside the church, a heavily armored knight guards the ground floor, and a robed caster on a balcony powers up enemies and throws magic. Kill the caster first if you can reach it.

Look for the elevator on the ground floor. Riding it opens a permanent shortcut to Firelink Shrine. From now on the trip from the bonfire to here takes seconds.

On the altar you’ll find a Fire Keeper Soul. Take it to Anastacia, the Fire Keeper at Firelink Shrine, to make your Estus Flask heal more.

3. Andre and the bonfire

Head down the stairs beside the church. You’ll hear a hammer: that’s Andre of Astora, the blacksmith, with a bonfire next to his forge.

  • Upgrade your weapon with Titanite Shards. Andre also sells them.
  • Buy the Crest of Artorias much later (it’s expensive). It opens a sealed door in Darkroot Garden.
  • Past Andre, a room with a Titanite Demon leads to Darkroot Garden. That’s an optional area for later; see Walkthrough Part 7.

4. Keys and Lautrec

  • Basement Key: found in the Parish. It opens a locked door back on the red drake’s bridge that leads down to the Lower Undead Burg (Part 5).
  • Mystery Key: found in the Parish. It opens the cell where Knight Lautrec of Carim is locked up. Freeing him is your choice: he’s helpful at first, but he has his own plans.

5. The Sunlight Altar

Near the bridge on the Parish side is the Sunlight Altar, where the Warriors of Sunlight gather. Solaire, who you met in the Undead Burg, is one of them. Faith builds can join once they meet the requirement and get the Lightning Spear miracle. See the Cleric build.

6. Up to the roof

Climb the ladders inside the church to the roof. At the top is a fog gate, and behind it the Bell Gargoyles. Read the Bell Gargoyles boss guide first. Solaire’s summon sign is near the fog gate if you spoke to him on the bridge.

After the fight, climb the bell tower and ring the bell. On your way back down, a merchant named Oswald of Carim waits in the tower.

Tips

  • Upgrade before the roof. Three or four upgrades on one weapon make the Gargoyles far easier.
  • There’s a Black Knight past the church. He hits very hard and drops good loot. Come back later if he’s too much.
  • Use the elevator. If you’re low on Estus, it’s faster to go back to Firelink than to push on.
