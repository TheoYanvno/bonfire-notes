Walkthrough Part 15: The Demon Ruins

Beneath Quelaag’s lair in Blighttown lie the Demon Ruins, what’s left of a city swallowed by fire. The demons you fought one at a time earlier in the game now roam here in groups. It’s the first half of the road to the Bed of Chaos and its Lord Soul.
How to get in Down past Quelaag’s lair in Blighttown
Biggest danger Groups of Taurus and Capra demons; lava
Bring A weapon at +10 or higher, fire resistance
Recommended After the Lordvessel
Bosses Ceaseless Discharge, Demon Firesage, Centipede Demon
Goal Get the Orange Charred Ring and reach Lost Izalith

1. The Fair Lady and the way down

Past Quelaag’s arena, a passage leads to a bonfire beside a sick woman, the Daughter of Chaos (Quelaag’s sister), and her servant Eingyi. From their chamber, the way continues down into the ruins. Don’t attack the woman; she’s harmless, and there’s a covenant here for those who give her humanity.

2. Ceaseless Discharge

The first part of the ruins is a long path above a sea of lava. On a ledge lies a body with the Gold-Hemmed Black set. Pick it up and a giant fire demon rises from the lava: Ceaseless Discharge. Read the Ceaseless Discharge boss guide. There’s a well-known way to win without fighting him head-on.

3. Demons everywhere

Past him, the ruins open up:

  • Taurus Demons and Capra Demons walk these halls, sometimes two at a time. Fight in doorways where they can’t reach you together.
  • Burrowing worms in the lava fields burst out of the ground.
  • Bonfires sit at a few safe corners. Find each one before pushing on.

4. Demon Firesage

A fog gate in the middle of the ruins leads to the Demon Firesage, a fiery version of the Asylum Demon. Read the Demon Firesage boss guide.

5. The Centipede Demon

Further down, at the edge of a lava lake, the Centipede Demon waits. Read the Centipede Demon boss guide. It drops the Orange Charred Ring, which greatly reduces lava damage. Without it, Lost Izalith’s lava fields are deadly.

6. The door to Lost Izalith

Past the Centipede Demon, a sealed door opens once you’ve placed the Lordvessel at Firelink Shrine. Behind it is Lost Izalith (Part 16).

Tips

  • Fire resistance matters. Armor or rings that reduce fire damage help everywhere here.
  • Pyromancers: most demons here resist fire. Use your weapon.
  • Don’t fight two demons at once. Retreat until only one follows you.
