Walkthrough Part 2: Firelink Shrine

The crow drops you at Firelink Shrine, a ruined shrine around a bonfire. You will come back here again and again: it connects to almost every early area. The trick is knowing which way to go first, because two of the three paths will kill a new character in seconds.

1. Light the bonfire and meet the locals

Rest at the bonfire. Then talk to the people around it:

  • The Crestfallen Warrior sits by the wall near the bonfire. He is gloomy, but he tells you the goal: ring two Bells of Awakening, one above and one below.
  • Petrus of Thorolund, a cleric, sits near the shrine. He offers the Way of White covenant and sells healing miracles.

2. Know your three paths

Path Where it goes Should you go now?
Up the stone stairs and along the aqueduct Undead Burg Yes. This is the intended route.
Down through the graveyard past the skeletons The Catacombs No. The skeletons keep coming back to life until you have the right weapon.
Down the long stairs past the shrine New Londo Ruins No. The ghosts there can’t be hurt without special items.

If enemies feel impossibly strong, you are probably on the wrong path. Turn around.

3. Grab the graveyard weapons (carefully)

The graveyard just below the shrine holds two early prizes:

  • Zweihander: a huge greatsword, excellent for Strength builds (it needs 24 Strength one-handed, or 16 if you two-hand it).
  • Winged Spear: a long spear with good reach.

Skeletons rise from the ground as you enter. Run in, grab the items and run back to the bonfire. Don’t stay to fight.

4. The Firekeeper

Below the shrine, behind a locked area you reach later, sits Anastacia, the Firekeeper. She tends the bonfire. Later you can bring her special items to strengthen your Estus Flask, and she can restore your human form.

5. Kindle the bonfire (when human)

While you are human (you use an item called Humanity at a bonfire to return to human form), you can kindle a bonfire. Kindling raises the Estus Flasks you get there from 5 to 10. Kindling the Firelink bonfire is one of the best uses of your first Humanity.

6. Head for the Undead Burg

Climb the stairs on the right side of the shrine area and follow the aqueduct up. That leads to the Undead Burg. Next: Walkthrough Part 3: the Undead Burg.

Tips

  • Firelink connects to everything. Later an elevator from the Undead Parish brings you straight back here.
  • Leave spare souls at home. There’s no bank, but spending souls on levels before exploring means you have less to lose.
  • Talk to everyone twice. Many characters say more the second time.
