Class Build: Pyromancer

The Pyromancer starts at level 1, the lowest in the game, with a Hand Axe, a cracked shield and a Pyromancy Flame that casts Fireball. That sounds weak, but it’s a secret strength: pyromancies get stronger when you upgrade the flame, not when you raise stats. So every level-up point can go into health, stamina and melee.
Starting level 1
Stats Vitality 10 · Attunement 12 · Endurance 11 · Strength 12 · Dexterity 9 · Resistance 12 · Intelligence 10 · Faith 8
Weapon Hand Axe
Shield Cracked Round Shield
Catalyst Pyromancy Flame (casts from your hand, so you can still hold a weapon)
Spell Fireball
Armor Tattered Cloth set and Heavy Boots
Plays like Melee fighter with a fireball for anything at range

In one line: one of the easiest classes for beginners, because of fire.

What the Pyromancer is good at

  • Damage from your stats-free flame. Upgrading the Pyromancy Flame raises every fire spell.
  • A melee build and a caster at the same time. The flame goes in your off hand.
  • Weaknesses to know: a few enemies resist fire, like Quelaag and much of Lost Izalith.

What to level up

Stat Early (to the Taurus Demon) Mid (to the Gaping Dragon) Late (Anor Londo) Goal
Vitality 16 25 30 40
Endurance 16 25 30 40
Strength 14 16 20 24
Dexterity 10 12 14 16
Attunement 12 14 16 16
  • Attunement 14 to 16 gives 3 to 4 spell slots, enough for the pyromancies you’ll actually use.
  • Intelligence and Faith do nothing for pyromancy. Leave them.

Upgrading the flame and new spells

What Where
Upgrade the Pyromancy Flame Laurentius, trapped in a barrel in the Depths (in the room past the Butcher). Free him and he moves to Firelink Shrine, where he upgrades the flame for souls.
Fireball, Fire Orb, Combustion, Iron Flesh, Flash Sweat Laurentius
Stronger pyromancies Quelana, his teacher, hiding near Quelaag’s Domain in Blighttown once your flame is well upgraded

Combustion is a close-range burst with huge damage early on, and Fire Orb is a stronger Fireball. Together they carry you a long way.

Weapons

Weapon Where Why use it
Hand Axe Starting gear Good enough while the flame does the heavy lifting.
Claymore Undead Burg, the bridge the red drake guards A big upgrade once you have 16 Strength.
Drake Sword Cut or shoot the red drake’s tail from under the Undead Burg bridge Strong early damage with no stat scaling needed.

Armor, shield and rings

Slot Early Later
Armor Tattered Cloth set Elite Knight set (Darkroot Garden) or anything mid-weight
Shield Cracked Round Shield A shield with full physical block, like the Heater Shield or Grass Crest Shield
Rings Havel’s Ring for heavier armor Wolf Ring for poise

How to play it

  1. Open fights with a Fireball, then finish with your weapon.
  2. Save Combustion for big, slow enemies right in front of you.
  3. Upgrade the flame first, before your weapon. It raises every spell at once.
