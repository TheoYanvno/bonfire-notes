New to Dark Souls? This page lists every guide on the blog in the order you will need them. Pick a build, then follow the walkthroughs and boss guides side by side.

1. Pick a build

Build Play style Difficulty
Strength Knight Heavy armor, big shield, huge hits Easiest
Pyromancer Melee plus fireballs; all points into survival Easy
Dexterity Swordsman Light armor, fast weapons, dodging Medium
Sorcerer Ranged magic, fragile but powerful Harder

Three rules that fix most problems

  1. Level Vitality and Endurance. Health and stamina save more runs than damage.
  2. Upgrade one weapon. Andre in the Undead Parish turns a good weapon into a great one.
  3. If an area feels impossible, you may be on the wrong path. Turn around and explore somewhere else.
