New to Dark Souls? This page lists every guide on the blog in the order you will need them. Pick a class, then follow the walkthroughs and boss guides side by side, from the Asylum to the Kiln of the First Flame.

1. Pick a class

Every class can finish the game. The class only decides where you start. Each guide covers the starting stats, the weapons and gear to look for, and what to level.

Class Starts with Play style Difficulty

Warrior Balanced Strength and Dexterity, a great early shield Quality build, any weapon Easy

Three rules that fix most problems

  1. Level Vitality and Endurance. Health and stamina save more runs than damage.
  2. Upgrade one weapon. Andre in the Undead Parish turns a good weapon into a great one.
  3. If an area feels impossible, you may be on the wrong path. Turn around and explore somewhere else.
