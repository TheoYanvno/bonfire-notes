Walkthrough Part 10: The Painted World of Ariamis

The Painted World of Ariamis is a snowy, cold world inside a painting in Anor Londo. It’s optional, and a little eerie: a place where unwanted things were locked away. It has great loot, an undead dragon and one of the gentlest bosses in the series.
How to get in Carry the Peculiar Doll and touch the big painting in Anor Londo
Biggest danger Crow-headed demons, and hollows that burst into worms
Recommended After Ornstein and Smough
Rewards The Annex area’s gear, and Priscilla’s Dagger if you cut her tail

1. The Peculiar Doll

The doll is back in the Northern Undead Asylum, where the game began. To get there, curl up on the ledge near the giant tree at Firelink Shrine and a crow carries you back. See the Stray Demon boss guide, because something waits for you in the Asylum too.

2. Inside the painting

You fall into a snowy courtyard of a crumbling castle.

  • Rotting hollows shuffle around. Some swell and burst into worms when hit. Back away when one starts to bulge.
  • Crow demons with crow heads swoop in from above. They hit hard but have little health.
  • Skeletons on wheels roll down some paths. Get out of their way, then hit them when they stop.

3. The bonfire and the bridge

There’s a bonfire inside the castle. From there, a long bridge leads across. An undead dragon waits beneath part of the world; you’ll recognize it by its rotting body. Its breath poisons, so fight from the side.

4. The Annex Key

Keep exploring the castle and you’ll find the Annex Key. It opens a locked area with extra loot and some of the toughest enemies in the painting.

5. Priscilla

At the end is the fog gate of Crossbreed Priscilla, a tall, pale half-dragon with a scythe. She doesn’t want to fight. She’ll ask you to leave. You can. If you attack, read the Priscilla boss guide first. Cutting her tail gives Priscilla’s Dagger.

6. Getting out

To leave the painting, jump off the edge at the spot where you first arrived. It returns you to Anor Londo.

Tips

  • Read item descriptions if you like lore: the things you find hint at why this world was painted.
  • Bring fire for the bursting hollows and the worms.
  • Don’t feel bad about leaving Priscilla alone. Many players never fight her.
