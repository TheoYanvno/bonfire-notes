Class Build: Deprived

The Deprived starts with almost nothing: a Club, a Plank Shield and no armor at all. Every stat is 11. It sounds like a joke, but it’s the most efficient class in the game if you already know what you want to build, because no point is ever “wasted” on a stat you didn’t choose. This guide builds a Strength fighter, the easiest path from a Deprived start, and lists how to turn it into anything else.
Starting level 6
Stats 11 in every stat
Weapon Club
Shield Plank Shield
Armor None
Plays like Whatever you build

In one line: the class for your second playthrough, or for a first one if you like a challenge.

What the Deprived is good at

  • Flexibility. Strength, Dexterity, magic or Faith: the Deprived is equally (un)prepared for all of them.
  • A low total level later. Because no stat starts high, a finished build reaches its targets at about the same level as any other class.
  • The downside: the first hour. No armor and a club. Pick up gear from the very first areas.

What to level up (Strength fighter)

Stat Early (to the Taurus Demon) Mid (to the Gaping Dragon) Late (Anor Londo) Goal
Vitality 18 25 30 40
Endurance 16 25 30 40
Strength 16 24 32 40
Dexterity 11 12 14 14

Other builds from a Deprived start:

  • Dexterity: swap the Strength column for Dexterity and follow the Wanderer weapon list.
  • Sorcery: raise Intelligence and Attunement; buy a catalyst and spells from Griggs. See the Sorcerer.
  • Faith: raise Faith and Attunement; buy a talisman from Petrus. See the Cleric.

Weapons

Weapon Where Why use it
Club Starting gear Surprisingly decent damage. Upgrade it once or twice while you look for better.
Drake Sword Shoot the red drake’s tail from under the Undead Burg bridge until it falls off (you’ll need a bow and arrows) A big early jump in damage.
Claymore Undead Burg, the bridge the red drake guards The best early Strength sword.
Zweihander Firelink Shrine graveyard Huge damage; two-hand it.

Armor and shield, fast

You start naked, so grab armor early:

  • The Undead Burg merchant sells a Chain Helm and Chain Armor, solid early protection.
  • Hollow soldiers in the Undead Burg can drop their gear.
  • A real shield: buy or find one with 100% physical block as soon as you can. The Plank Shield lets a lot through.

Later, the Elite Knight set in Darkroot Garden and the Grass Crest Shield in Darkroot Basin are great for any build.

Rings

  • Havel’s Ring (Havel the Rock, Undead Burg watchtower) for heavy armor.
  • Wolf Ring (Darkroot Garden) for poise.

Starting gift tip

Take the Black Firebombs. They’re the strongest early damage in the game, they make the Taurus Demon much easier, and the Deprived needs all the help it can get in the first hour.
