Walkthrough Part 12: The Great Hollow and Ash Lake

guidewalkthrough

  4. Tips

Hidden at the bottom of Blighttown is one of the most memorable places in Dark Souls: a giant hollow tree you climb down inside, and at its roots, Ash Lake, a gray sea under endless stone trees. It’s optional, but you should see it.
How to get in An illusory wall in Blighttown’s swamp
Biggest danger Falling, and curse from the frog-like creatures
Bring Purging Stones (cure curse), patience
Recommended Any time after Blighttown
Rewards Ash Lake, a covenant, and some rare items

1. Finding the entrance

In the Blighttown swamp, near a huge hollow tree, there’s a wall that isn’t real. Hit or roll into the walls around the tree’s base until one disappears. Behind it is a bonfire and the start of the Great Hollow.

2. Down the tree

The Great Hollow is a long climb down through the inside of a tree, along huge branches and roots.

  • Look before every drop. Most branches lead to the next one; some lead to nothing.
  • Mushroom people and crystal lizards live here.
  • Creatures that breathe curse (they look like big frogs with bulging eyes) live in the lower parts. If curse builds up it kills you, and while cursed your maximum health is halved. Kill them fast or keep away, and carry a Purging Stone.

3. Ash Lake

At the bottom, the tree opens onto Ash Lake: a beach of gray sand, a still sea and giant stone trees stretching up into darkness. There’s a bonfire on the shore.

  • Two Hydras live in the water. Fight them close to the shore, where you can reach their heads, and stay out of their water spray.
  • Walk the beach to the end and you’ll find the Everlasting Dragon, one of the last ancient dragons. It doesn’t fight. Talk to it to join the Path of the Dragon covenant.

Tips

  • Rest at every bonfire you find on the way down.
  • Bring cures. Curse is the real danger here, not the enemies.
  • It’s a long walk back up. With the Lordvessel you can warp out from a bonfire instead.
