The Undead Burg is the first real test in Dark Souls. Hollow soldiers come in groups, some throw firebombs from above, and there is a boss waiting on a narrow wall. Go slowly, pull enemies one at a time, and learn the shortcuts.

1. Climb up from the aqueduct

From Firelink, climb the stairs and follow the aqueduct up into the Burg. Your first enemies are hollow soldiers with swords and shields, and a few with crossbows.

Golden rule here: if you see two enemies, back away until only one follows you.

2. Find the bonfire

The first bonfire in the Burg is tucked under a staircase near the start of the area. Find it and rest before going further. Everything that follows is easier when you know where your checkpoint is.

3. Watch the rooftops

Hollows on the rooftops throw firebombs at you. Look up as you walk. Rush them when they stop to throw, or take a different route around their line of sight.

4. The rooftop merchant

Across the rooftops, a hunched merchant sits by himself. He is worth finding:

Item Why buy it
Firebombs Excellent against the Taurus Demon and early groups
Residence Key Opens a locked house in the Lower Undead Burg where the sorcerer Griggs is trapped
Uchigatana A strong bleeding katana for Dexterity builds
Throwing Knives Pull enemies one at a time from far away

5. The wall and the Taurus Demon

Climb the tower and cross the long stone wall. Two hollow archers shoot from the tower behind you. When you walk through the fog at the end, the Taurus Demon leaps onto the wall.

The secret: climb the ladder in the tower right behind you and drop onto the demon for a plunging attack. Full strategy in the Taurus Demon boss guide.

6. Havel’s tower (come back later)

After the Taurus Demon, the tower has a locked basement door. Behind it is Havel the Rock, a heavily armored enemy who hits incredibly hard. He drops Havel’s Ring and his armor set, but leave him until you are much stronger.

7. Solaire and the red dragon bridge

Climb the stairs toward the long bridge. Standing in the sunlight is Solaire of Astora. Talk to him: he gives you the White Sign Soapstone, so you can help other players, and you can summon him for some boss fights later.

The first time you step onto the bridge, a red dragon lands at the far end and burns it with fire. Back away and wait for it to fly off, then sprint across. Don’t stop to fight. The dragon is not meant to be beaten now.

8. On to the Undead Parish

Across the bridge is the Undead Parish and its church. The Bell Gargoyles guard the first Bell of Awakening on the roof. Read the Bell Gargoyles boss guide before you climb up.

Tips

  • Upgrade your weapon. The blacksmith Andre lives in the Undead Parish. Two or three upgrades make the Burg much easier.
  • Shortcuts appear as you go. Doors that won’t open from one side often open from the other. Mark them in your head.
  • Farm a little if you’re stuck. The hollow soldiers near the bonfire give steady souls for a level or two.
