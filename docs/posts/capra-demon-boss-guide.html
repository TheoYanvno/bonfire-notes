Boss Guide 4: Capra Demon

guidebosses

In this post

  2. Kill the dogs first
  3. Then the demon
  4. After the fight

The Capra Demon is famous for killing players in the first five seconds. The room is tiny, and the demon and two dogs attack the moment you walk in. The fix is simple once you know it: don’t stay where you enter.
Where Lower Undead Burg
Enemies Capra Demon plus two dogs
Reward Key to the Depths and souls
Recommended Weapon at +5, 20 or more Vitality, a shield that blocks well

The first five seconds

  1. As soon as you pass the fog, run straight ahead and slightly to the left.
  2. Run up the stairs onto the stone wall at the left side of the room.
  3. Turn around at the top. The dogs follow you up the narrow stairs one at a time.

Don’t stop to look around. Don’t heal. Just go.

Kill the dogs first

On the stairs, the dogs can only reach you one at a time. Block and kill both dogs before worrying about the demon. They have little health, and they’re far more dangerous than they look because they interrupt your attacks and heals.

Then the demon

Attack What it looks like How to avoid it
Double sword slash Rapid swings with both blades Block with a good shield, or roll back
Jumping slam Leaps at you and slams down Roll to the side
Combo Several swings in a row Back off and wait for it to finish
  • Plunging attack: from the top of the wall, drop onto the demon when it walks below you.
  • Fight it on the stairs or the wall where it has less room, and hit it after each combo ends.
  • Pyromancers: Combustion does big damage while it recovers from a swing.

After the fight

It drops the Key to the Depths. The door it opens is near the Capra Demon’s area in the Lower Undead Burg, and it leads down into the Depths. Next boss: Gaping Dragon.
