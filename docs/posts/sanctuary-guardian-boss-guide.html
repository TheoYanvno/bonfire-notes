Boss Guide 22: Sanctuary Guardian

guidebossesdlc

In this post
  1. Its attacks
  2. Strategy
  3. After the fight

The Sanctuary Guardian is the first thing waiting for you in the past, right after you arrive in the DLC. It’s a winged, lion-like beast with a scorpion-like tail, and it hits hard and fast. Stay at its side, watch for the lightning, and go for the tail.
Where Sanctuary Garden, the first area of the DLC
Reward Souls, and the Guardian Tail if you cut its tail
Recommended Weapon at +12 to +15, 35 Vitality

Its attacks

Attack What it looks like How to avoid it
Lightning breath Lightning from its mouth in a line Move to its side
Pounce Leaps at you from a distance Roll sideways as it lands
Tail stab The tail strikes from over its back Keep moving; don’t stand still in front
Headbutt and claws Quick attacks in front Stay at its side, not its face

Strategy

  1. Get to its side and stay there. Most attacks go forward.
  2. Hit two or three times, then back off. It recovers quickly.
  3. Don’t stand at long range. It closes the gap with its pounce.
  4. Cut the tail for the Guardian Tail, a whip-like weapon.

After the fight
