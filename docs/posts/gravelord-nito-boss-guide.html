Boss Guide 15: Gravelord Nito

guidebosses

In this post
  1. His attacks
  2. Strategy
  3. After the fight

Gravelord Nito is one of the Lords from the opening of the game: a giant made of countless skeletons, ruler of death. He waits at the bottom of the Tomb of the Giants with skeletons all around him. Deal with the skeletons first, then focus on Nito.
Where The bottom of the Tomb of the Giants
Reward A Lord Soul (for the Lordvessel)
Recommended Weapon at +10 to +15, 35 Vitality, a light source for the way down

His attacks

Attack What it looks like How to avoid it
Sword sweeps His huge sword swings low across the ground Roll toward him, through the swing
Overhead strike Raises the sword and slams it down Roll sideways
Miasma Raises his arms; a poison cloud bursts around him Run away until it’s gone; it builds toxic fast
Grab Grabs and crushes you Don’t stand right in front of him

Strategy

  1. Pull the skeletons away first. Run to the side of the arena; the skeletons follow you, Nito is slower.
  2. A Divine weapon stops the skeletons getting back up. Without one, kill them and move on before they rise.
  3. Fight Nito at his feet, hitting his legs, and roll through his sweeps.
  4. When he raises his arms, leave. The poison cloud is the real danger.
  5. Heal away from him. His reach is long.

After the fight

Place the Lord Soul in the Lordvessel. Kindled bonfires in the Tomb make the long walk easier if you come back. Back to the guides hub.
