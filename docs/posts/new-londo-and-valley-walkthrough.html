Walkthrough Part 11: New Londo Ruins and the Valley of Drakes

guidewalkthrough

In this post
  1. New Londo Ruins
  2. The Valley of Drakes
  3. Tips

New Londo Ruins is a drowned city below Firelink Shrine. Long ago it was flooded on purpose to stop the Darkwraiths, and the water still holds them back. To reach the Four Kings, one of the bosses holding a Lord Soul, you have to drain it. Next to it lies the Valley of Drakes, a ravine full of undead dragons that connects New Londo with Darkroot and Blighttown.
Biggest danger Ghosts you can’t hurt normally; Darkwraiths below
Bring Transient Curse items (or be cursed) to hit ghosts; the Covenant of Artorias ring for the Abyss
Recommended After the Lordvessel
Goal Drain the city, enter the Abyss and beat the Four Kings

New Londo Ruins

1. Getting in

From Firelink Shrine, go down past the lower part of the shrine to the long elevator that drops into the ruins. A blacksmith named Rickert of Vinheim is chained up here; he upgrades weapons down the magic paths.

2. The ghosts

New Londo is haunted. Ghosts pass through walls and can’t be hit by normal weapons. To fight them you need to be cursed:

  • Use a Transient Curse (dropped by the ghosts, and found around the ruins) to curse yourself briefly.
  • Or be cursed for real, which also halves your maximum health until you’re cured.
  • Some weapons can hit ghosts by themselves.

Without any of these, run past them toward the exits.

3. Ingward and the seal

Climb to the top of the tower in the ruins and you’ll find Ingward, an old sealer who guards the city’s seal. Talk to him and he gives you the Key to the Seal, which opens the gate that drains the water.

4. The drained city

With the water gone, the lower streets are open, and so are the Darkwraiths, black-armored knights who can grab you and steal your humanity. Fight them one at a time, and don’t let them grab you: block or roll when one reaches out.

5. The Abyss and the Four Kings

At the bottom is a dark hole: the Abyss. Don’t jump in without the Covenant of Artorias ring (from Sif). Without it, the Abyss kills you instantly. With it, you land in the arena of the Four Kings. Read the Four Kings boss guide.

The Valley of Drakes

6. A dragon ravine

The Valley of Drakes connects New Londo, Darkroot Basin (through a door that needs the Master Key) and the top of Blighttown. Undead dragons, huge rotting bodies, lie across the paths. They’re slow but hit extremely hard and breathe poison.

  • Kill them from behind, or pull them one at a time.
  • Narrow bridges and cliffs: a drake’s wing swipe can knock you off.

7. Rings and a way into Blighttown

The valley hides the Red Tearstone Ring, which boosts your damage when your health is low. At the far end, a tunnel leads to the upper part of Blighttown, the long way round to Quelaag.

Tips

  • The ghosts look scary but are weak once you can hit them.
  • Don’t drain the city before you’re ready for Darkwraiths. They’re tough at low level.
  • Get the Covenant of Artorias first. It’s the only way to survive the Abyss.
