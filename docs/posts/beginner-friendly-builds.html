Five Beginner-Friendly Builds

In Dark Souls, your starting class only decides your first few levels and your starting gear. Any class can become anything. Still, some play styles are friendlier for a first playthrough.

1. The Knight (sword and board)

Why: The Knight starts with strong armor, a solid sword and a good shield. You can block most early attacks and learn enemy patterns safely.

Level: Vitality (health), Endurance (stamina and equip load), then Strength.

Play style: Shield up, wait for an opening, strike once or twice, shield up again.

2. The Pyromancer

Why: Pyromancers throw fireballs that grow stronger as you upgrade the Pyromancy Flame, not your stats. That means you can put every level into health and stamina and still hit hard.

Level: Vitality and Endurance first. Add Attunement for more spell slots.

Tip: Look for the fellow pyromancer in the Depths. Helping him lets you upgrade your flame.

3. The Strength build

Why: Big, slow weapons stagger enemies and break their guard. Simple and satisfying.

Level: Strength, Endurance, Vitality.

Gear: A greatsword or large axe. Two-handing a weapon gives you a strength bonus, so you can wield heavy weapons earlier.

4. The Dexterity build

Why: Fast weapons like katanas and straight swords let you attack quickly and get out of danger.

Level: Dexterity, Endurance, Vitality.

Tip: A merchant in the Undead Burg sells a katana early on, if you can save up the souls.

5. The Sorcerer

Why: Fighting from a distance is safer, and magic hits the weak spots of many armored enemies.

Level: Intelligence and Attunement first, then Vitality.

Tip: Rescue the sorcerer locked away in the Undead Burg to learn more spells. And always keep a melee weapon for close calls.

The secret build

Whatever you pick, the real build is Vitality and Endurance. More health and stamina will save more runs than any damage number. You can always respec your plan as you go, so experiment and have fun.
