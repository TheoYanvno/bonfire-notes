Boss Guide 1: Asylum Demon

The Asylum Demon is a huge, slow brute with a giant hammer. It is the first boss in Dark Souls, and it is designed to teach you two things: you can’t win every fight right away, and attacking from above is powerful.
Where Northern Undead Asylum courtyard
Health Moderate (it’s a tutorial boss)
Weak to Plunging attacks from the balcony
Reward Big Pilgrim’s Key (lets you leave the Asylum) and souls
Recommended Your class weapon and shield, Estus Flask from Oscar

The first meeting: run

The first time the demon drops into the courtyard, you only have a broken sword hilt. Run for the small open door on the left. A gate closes behind you. This is the intended path. Go get your gear and the Estus Flask first (Asylum walkthrough).

The second meeting: drop on its head

You come back on a balcony above the courtyard. The demon is waiting below.

  1. Walk off the ledge toward the demon.
  2. Press attack while falling. You land a plunging attack that takes a large part of its health, often more than a quarter.
  3. Immediately lock on and start the real fight.

Its attacks

Attack What it looks like How to avoid it
Hammer smash Raises the hammer high, slams it down Roll to the side, toward its back
Side sweep Swings the hammer across in front Roll through it or stay behind
Jump and butt slam Leaps up and lands on its rear Move away when it crouches to jump; it can hit behind it too
Ground pound Short hop and stomp Back off a few steps

Strategy

  1. Stay behind its left leg (your right as you face it). The hammer is in its right hand, so it struggles to hit you there.
  2. Hit two or three times, then back off and watch for the jump.
  3. Don’t block the hammer with a small shield; it breaks your guard. Rolling is safer.
  4. Heal when it jumps away, not while it’s next to you.

After the fight

Pick up the Big Pilgrim’s Key, rest at the bonfire, and open the big doors. Outside, a crow carries you to Lordran. Next boss: Taurus Demon.
