Class Build: Bandit

The Bandit is raw power. It starts with Strength 14 and Endurance 14, the highest of any class, plus a Battle Axe and a Spider Shield. This guide builds a pure Strength brawler who uses the heaviest weapons in the game and staggers enemies with every swing.
Starting level 4
Stats Vitality 12 · Attunement 8 · Endurance 14 · Strength 14 · Dexterity 9 · Resistance 11 · Intelligence 8 · Faith 10
Weapon Battle Axe
Shield Spider Shield (blocks poison well)
Armor The Brigand set (hood, armor, gauntlets, trousers)
Plays like Big, slow swings that knock enemies over

In one line: the class that gets to the big weapons first.

What the Bandit is good at

  • Big weapons early. With Strength 14, two-handing gives you an effective 21 Strength right away.
  • Stamina. Endurance 14 means more swings and blocks than any other class at the start.
  • Wasted stats are low. Attunement 8, Intelligence 8 and Dexterity 9 are the ones a Strength build doesn’t need.

Everything else leave it leave it leave it leave it
  • Strength 16 right away for the Claymore, 24 to one-hand the Zweihander, then on toward 40.
  • Dexterity 10 is the minimum for most swords; only raise it if a weapon you want needs more.

Weapons

Weapon Where Why use it
Battle Axe Starting gear (also sold by Andre and the Undead Burg merchant) Solid damage while you level.
Claymore Undead Burg, on the bridge the red drake guards Great sweeps for groups.
Zweihander Firelink Shrine graveyard Massive damage and stagger; two-hand it.
Black Knight Greataxe Rare drop from Black Knights Enormous hits for a pure Strength build.

Upgrade with Andre in the Undead Parish. Heavy weapons are slow, so learn each one’s swing timing before trusting it on a boss.

Armor, shield and rings

Slot Early Later
Armor Brigand set Elite Knight set (Darkroot Garden) or heavier pieces as Endurance allows
Shield Spider Shield (great in Blighttown’s poison) Grass Crest Shield (Darkroot Basin)
Rings Havel’s Ring (Undead Burg watchtower): +50% equip load Wolf Ring (Darkroot Garden): more poise

How to play it

  1. Two-hand most of the time. Your Strength counts as 1.5×, and big swings stagger enemies out of their attacks.
  2. Use the running attack to open fights; it reaches far with heavy weapons.
  3. Don’t swing into a group. Pull enemies one at a time to the doorway.
  4. Mind your stamina. Heavy swings cost a lot; stop with enough left to roll.
