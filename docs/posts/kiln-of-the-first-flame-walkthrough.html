Walkthrough Part 18: The Kiln of the First Flame

When the Lordvessel holds all four Lord Souls (Nito, the Bed of Chaos, Seath, and the Four Kings), the way to the Kiln of the First Flame opens. It’s short, quiet and sad: a gray field of ash where the First Flame is dying, and the one who has been keeping it alive.
How to get in Place the four Lord Souls in the Lordvessel, then go through the Firelink Altar
Biggest danger Black Knights
Recommended Weapon at +12 to +15, 35+ Vitality
Goal Beat Gwyn and choose an ending

1. Before you go

This is the point of no return. After the final boss, the game starts again in New Game Plus with tougher enemies. Before you go:

  • Finish anything you want in this playthrough: optional bosses, NPC stories, covenants.
  • Spend your souls. Upgrade your weapon and armor.
  • Pick up any upgrade materials you’ve been saving.

2. The path of ash

The way into the Kiln leads down long stairs into a field of ash and ruined stone.

Black Knights guard it, the same tall knights in charred armor you’ve met all over Lordran. They hit very hard.

  • Fight them one at a time.
  • Block, then counter, or roll behind their swings.
  • They drop strong weapons, so take your time.

3. The fog gate

At the end of the field is the Kiln itself and its fog gate. Inside waits Gwyn, Lord of Cinder. Read the Gwyn boss guide. The music alone tells you this fight is different.

4. The ending

After Gwyn, a bonfire appears in the middle of the Kiln. What you do now decides the ending:

  • Link the Fire: rest at the bonfire and keep the Age of Fire going, as Gwyn did.
  • The Dark Lord: walk away from the bonfire and let the fire fade. The serpent Kaathe guides players toward this ending.

There’s no wrong choice. Read The Age of Fire, Explained for what each one means.

Tips

  • There is no shortcut. If you die, you walk the ash field again, so keep the Black Knights in mind.
  • Parrying works on Gwyn. If you can parry, learn it before this fight.
