The Thief is built for critical hits. It starts with the highest Dexterity in the game (15), the Bandit’s Knife (a dagger with huge critical damage), a Target Shield for parrying, and a Master Key, which opens many locked doors and shortcuts early. This guide builds a backstab and parry specialist.
Starting level 5
Stats Vitality 9 · Attunement 11 · Endurance 9 · Strength 9 · Dexterity 15 · Resistance 10 · Intelligence 12 · Faith 11
Weapon Bandit’s Knife
Shield Target Shield (made for parrying)
Armor Thief Mask and the Black Leather set
Item Master Key (so you can choose a different starting gift)
Plays like Get behind enemies, or parry them, and finish with a critical hit

In one line: the class with the hardest start and the biggest critical hits.

What the Thief is good at

  • Critical hits. Backstabs and parry ripostes with the Bandit’s Knife hit far harder than its normal attacks.
  • The Master Key. It opens doors you’d otherwise need keys for, like shortcuts in the Undead Burg and the way into the Valley of Drakes. And since the Thief already has it, you can take a different gift, such as the Black Firebombs.
  • The downside: Vitality 9 and Endurance 9 are the lowest in the game. Your first levels must go into health and stamina.

  • Ten levels into Vitality and Endurance before anything else. A Thief at Vitality 9 dies to almost everything.
  • Dexterity to 40 for criticals, and a little Strength for better shields.

Weapons

Weapon Where Why use it
Bandit’s Knife Starting gear Huge criticals. Keep it even after you switch main weapons, just for backstabs.
Balder Side Sword Balder Knights, Undead Parish Thrusts through shields; a great main weapon.
Painting Guardian Sword Painting Guardians, Anor Londo Very fast, long chains of slashes.
Silver Knight Straight Sword Silver Knights, Anor Londo Strong late-game Dexterity sword.

Armor, shield and rings

Slot Early Later
Armor Thief and Black Leather pieces Stay light: under 25% equip load for the fastest roll
Shield Target Shield (best for parrying) Keep a parry shield; add a sturdier shield for blocking if you like
Rings Hornet Ring (behind Artorias’s grave, past Sif): much bigger critical hits Dark Wood Grain Ring (dropped by Shiva of the East’s bodyguard): a faster roll

How to play it

  1. Parry practice: the Target Shield parries early, so you have more time to react. Parry the Undead Burg’s hollow soldiers until it feels natural.
  2. Backstab slow enemies. Walk around their attack, get behind them and press attack.
  3. Use the Master Key to make shortcuts early, but don’t wander into areas far above your level.
  4. Throwing knives and firebombs cover the Thief’s weak ranged game.
