Boss Guide 20: Seath the Scaleless

guidebosses

In this post
  1. His attacks
  2. Strategy
  3. After the fight

Seath the Scaleless is the dragon who betrayed his own kind and sided with Gwyn. He has no scales, so he can’t die naturally, and the Primordial Crystal in his cave keeps him immortal. Break the crystal first; then he can be killed.
Where The Crystal Cave, past the Duke’s Archives
Watch out for Curse from his crystal breath. Carry a Purging Stone
Reward Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (for the Lordvessel), and the Moonlight Greatsword if you cut his tail
Recommended Weapon at +12 to +15, 35 Vitality

His attacks

Attack What it looks like How to avoid it
Crystal breath A cloud of crystal from his mouth Get out of the cloud; it builds curse
Crystal spikes Crystals erupt from the ground Keep moving when he slams down
Tentacle swipes His arm-like tentacles sweep Stay at his side or back
Tail sweep Sweeps his tail behind him Hit the tail, then step back

Strategy

  1. Run straight to the Primordial Crystal at the start and break it. Until you do, he can’t die.
  2. Stay behind him. His breath and spikes go forward.
  3. Cut his tail if you want the Moonlight Greatsword. It’s best for Intelligence builds.
  4. Watch the curse meter. If it fills, you die and your maximum health is halved until it’s cured. Get out of the breath early.

After the fight

That’s another Lord Soul. If you cut the tail, try the Moonlight Greatsword: it fires a magic wave with heavy attacks. Back to the guides hub.
