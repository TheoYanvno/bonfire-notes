Boss Guide 12: Great Grey Wolf Sif

guidebosses

In this post
  1. Its attacks
  2. Strategy
  3. After the fight

Great Grey Wolf Sif guards the grave of the knight Artorias in Darkroot Garden. Sif fights with Artorias’s greatsword in its mouth. It’s fast and hits hard, but every attack has a tell. Stay near its side or back legs, and attack after its big moves.
Where Darkroot Garden, behind the door the Crest of Artorias opens
Reward Covenant of Artorias ring (needed for the Abyss and the Four Kings) and Soul of Sif
Recommended Weapon at +10, 30 Vitality

Its attacks

Attack What it looks like How to avoid it
Sword swipe Quick slashes in front Stay at its side
Spin Leans and spins the sword in a full circle Roll backward, then hit it as it finishes
Leap back and charge Jumps away, then dashes at you with the blade Roll sideways just before it reaches you
Jump slam Leaps into the air and lands blade first Move away from where it’s going to land

Strategy

  1. Lock on and stay at its side, circling as it turns. Most of its swipes hit what’s in front of it.
  2. After each combo or spin, hit it two or three times in the side or back legs.
  3. When it jumps away, get ready to roll sideways. The charge comes straight at you.
  4. Fight in the open. Gravestones can trap your roll.
  5. Near the end, it limps. It’s harder to hit while limping, but it’s almost over.

After the fight

Put on the Covenant of Artorias only when you need it: it lets you walk in the Abyss in New Londo to reach the Four Kings. Behind the grave is the Hornet Ring. Back to the guides hub.
