Walkthrough Part 8: Sen's Fortress

Ring both Bells of Awakening and the gate of Sen’s Fortress opens, at the top of the Undead Parish. Sen’s is a test built by the gods to see who’s worthy of reaching their city. It’s one giant trap: slow down, look before you step, and nothing here is fair until you know where it is.
Biggest danger Swinging blades over narrow walkways, and rolling boulders
Bring A bow or throwing knives for the archers
Recommended Weapon at +7 to +10, 25+ Vitality, decent poise
Goal Reach the roof and beat the Iron Golem

1. The gate and Siegmeyer

Outside the gate you’ll meet Siegmeyer of Catarina, a cheerful knight in onion-shaped armor who’s waiting for the gate to open. Talk to him; you’ll keep running into him later.

2. The blade walkways

Just inside, narrow walkways cross over long drops, and huge pendulum blades swing across them.

  • Watch the rhythm. Walk up to each blade’s edge, wait for it to pass, then move to the gap between blades.
  • Don’t roll. A roll on a narrow beam often goes over the side.
  • Snake soldiers (half man, half serpent) wait at the ends. Fight them on solid floor, not on the walkway.

3. Traps everywhere

  • Pressure plates: some floor tiles fire arrows from the walls. Look for slightly raised squares.
  • Boulders: a giant on the roof rolls huge boulders down the ramps. When you hear rumbling, step into a side alcove and let it pass.
  • Mimics: some treasure chests are monsters that grab and eat you. If a chest looks like it’s breathing, or you’re not sure, hit it first before opening.

4. The bonfire

About halfway through, after the first set of ramps, there’s a bonfire. Rest there and treat everything before it as a route to memorize.

5. Logan and the merchant

  • Big Hat Logan, a famous sorcerer, is locked in a cage inside the fortress. Free him with the Cage Key and he’ll teach sorceries later.
  • A merchant sells titanite and armor in a side tower, reached by jumping a gap.

6. The roof

The way up winds around the outside of the fortress. At the top, the boulder giant throws firebombs at you from his platform, so keep moving. Past him is the fog gate of the Iron Golem. Read the Iron Golem boss guide. Black Iron Tarkus may be summoned for this fight if his sign is outside.

7. Carried away

After the Golem, a cutscene: bat-winged demons carry you off to Anor Londo, the city of the gods (Part 9).

Tips

  • Patience beats speed here. Almost every death in Sen’s is a trap you could see if you looked.
  • Lightning-throwing serpent mages stand on high ledges. Close the distance or shoot them.
  • The boulders reset when you rest. Each new attempt, remember where the alcoves are.
