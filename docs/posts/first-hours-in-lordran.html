Your First Hours in Lordran: 10 Beginner Tips

The first few hours of Dark Souls are the hardest, because you don’t know the rules yet. Here is what helps the most.

1. Bonfires are checkpoints with a cost

Resting at a bonfire refills your health and your Estus Flasks, and sets where you come back after dying. But it also brings most enemies back to life. Rest when you need to, not every time you pass one.

2. Your souls are waiting where you fell

Souls are both experience and money. When you die, you drop them all. Get back to that spot without dying and you pick them up again; die on the way and they’re gone. Spend souls before risky trips.

3. Raise your shield

Hold block while you walk toward enemies. It costs stamina, but it is far safer than trading hits. Let go of block between attacks so your stamina bar refills.

4. Watch your stamina, not your health

Attacking, rolling, running and blocking all use stamina. The most common beginner death is swinging until the stamina bar is empty and then being unable to roll away. Always keep a little in reserve.

5. Keep your equipment light

How fast you roll depends on how heavy your gear is compared to your limit. Stay under 25% for a fast roll, under 50% for a normal roll, and above that you’ll flop slowly. A lighter armor set often protects you better simply because you dodge more.

6. The graveyard is a trap

The path down past the graveyard near Firelink Shrine is full of skeletons that are much tougher than anything else nearby. The intended route is up, toward the Undead Burg. Come back to the graveyard later.

7. Upgrade one good weapon

A fully upgraded basic weapon beats a fancy weapon you can’t improve. Find the blacksmith in the Undead Parish and put your titanite into one weapon you like.

8. Lock on, but not always

Locking on keeps you facing the enemy, which is great in one-on-one fights. In groups or near ledges, unlocking gives you more control over where you roll.

9. Kindle your favorite bonfire

Being human lets you kindle a bonfire, which gives you more Estus each time you rest there. Pick the bonfire closest to a tough area or boss.

10. Read the messages, and summon help

Glowing messages on the floor often point out hidden items or warn about ambushes. If you are human, you can summon other players or friendly characters near many bosses. Using help isn’t cheating; it’s part of the game.

Above all: take breaks. Many players beat a boss on the first try after a good night’s sleep.
