Walkthrough Part 19 (DLC): Sanctuary Garden, Oolacile Sanctuary and the Royal Wood

Artorias of the Abyss is the expansion that comes with Dark Souls Remastered and the Prepare to Die edition. It sends you back in time to Oolacile, a land that’s about to be swallowed by the Abyss, and lets you meet the legendary knight Artorias you’ve heard about all game.
How to get in The Broken Pendant (Duke’s Archives) and a black mist in Darkroot Basin
Biggest danger Everything here hits hard; it’s balanced for the late game
Recommended After the Duke’s Archives, weapon at +15
Bosses Sanctuary Guardian, Knight Artorias, Black Dragon Kalameet

1. Getting there

  1. Darkroot Basin: beat the Hydra in the lake, then go to the alcove behind where it was. A crystal golem appears there after you leave and come back. Kill it to free Dusk of Oolacile and say yes to her.
  2. The Duke’s Archives: another crystal golem waits near the first bonfire. It drops the Broken Pendant.
  3. Back to the alcove: with the pendant, a black mist appears. Touch it, and you’re pulled into the past.

2. Sanctuary Garden

You land in a garden and walk straight into the first boss: the Sanctuary Guardian, a winged lion with a scorpion’s tail. Read the Sanctuary Guardian boss guide.

3. Oolacile Sanctuary

Past the Guardian is a small sanctuary with a bonfire and Elizabeth, a giant, kind mushroom who explains what’s happening to Oolacile. Rest and talk to her.

4. The Royal Wood

The Royal Wood is a darker, older version of Darkroot.

  • Scarecrows with farm tools swarm you. They’re weak but fast and come in groups.
  • Stone guardians with huge shields and maces block the paths. They’re slow; get behind them.
  • Crystal and golden golems guard parts of the wood.
  • A bonfire near the center connects everything.

Look for the Crest Key. It opens the door to Hawkeye Gough, a giant archer who was one of Gwyn’s knights. He’s the key to the Black Dragon fight.

5. Knight Artorias

An elevator near the end of the Royal Wood leads down to an arena where Knight Artorias, consumed by the Abyss, waits. This is one of the best boss fights in the series. Read the Artorias boss guide.

6. The Black Dragon

Before Artorias, a valley beside the wood is home to Black Dragon Kalameet, a dragon who will burn you from the sky. With Gough’s help you can fight him. Read the Kalameet boss guide.

Tips

  • Come late. The DLC is meant for the end of the game.
  • Talk to everyone. Elizabeth, Gough and others make the story click.
