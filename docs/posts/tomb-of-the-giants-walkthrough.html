Walkthrough Part 14: The Tomb of the Giants

The Tomb of the Giants is the darkest place in Dark Souls, literally. You can barely see your own feet, the paths run along the edges of bottomless drops, and giant skeletons guard them. At the very bottom lies Gravelord Nito, the first of the dead.
Biggest danger Darkness and falls; giant skeletons that knock you off ledges
Bring A light: the Skull Lantern, the Cast Light sorcery, or the Sunlight Maggot helmet
Recommended After the Catacombs and Pinwheel
Goal Reach the bottom and beat Gravelord Nito

1. Bring a light

Before anything else, sort out a light:

  • Skull Lantern: necromancers in the Tomb can drop it. Hold it in your off hand.
  • Cast Light: a sorcery from Big Hat Logan that lights up the area around you.
  • Sunlight Maggot: a glowing helmet from Lost Izalith, if you’ve been there first.

Without a light you’ll fall. With one, the Tomb becomes a tense but fair area.

2. The first bonfire and Patches

Near the start, beside a bonfire, you meet a cheerful man named Patches. Be careful near ledges when he’s around. If he pushes you into a pit, don’t panic; there’s a way out, and there’s a way to get even.

Rhea of Thorolund and her companions also passed through here on their way to find the Rite of Kindling. What happens to them depends on what you do.

3. Down the edges

The paths hug the walls with drops on the other side.

  • Giant skeletons carry huge weapons and can knock you off. Fight them with your back to a wall, not to the drop.
  • Skeleton beasts run at you in packs. Block, then hit.
  • Keep to walls and look at the ground before every step.

4. The second bonfire

Partway down, a second bonfire sits on a ledge. Kindle it. From here, the way to the boss is a series of drops.

5. The drop to Nito

At the bottom of the tomb, a jump into a lower chamber leads to Nito’s fog gate. Read the Gravelord Nito boss guide before you go. He drops the Lord Soul for the Lordvessel.

Tips

  • Turn your screen brightness up before you start.
  • Follow the walls. In the dark, the edge of a path is easier to find by touch than by sight.
  • Divine and blunt weapons make short work of the skeletons, just like in the Catacombs.
