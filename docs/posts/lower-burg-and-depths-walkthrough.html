Walkthrough Part 5: Lower Undead Burg and the Depths

guidewalkthrough

In this post
  1. Lower Undead Burg
  2. The Depths
  3. Tips

After the first bell, the way to the second bell is down. You go through the Lower Undead Burg, a cramped neighborhood full of ambushes, and then into the Depths, the sewers underneath. At the bottom waits the Gaping Dragon, and past it, Blighttown.
Biggest danger Dog ambushes in the Lower Burg; the Butcher and slimes in the Depths
Bring The Basement Key from the Undead Parish
Recommended Weapon at +5, 20 Vitality
Goal Beat the Capra Demon for the Key to the Depths, then the Gaping Dragon at the bottom

Lower Undead Burg

1. The way down

Back on the red drake’s bridge in the Undead Burg, the Basement Key from the Parish opens a locked door. Stairs and a long ladder bring you down into the Lower Burg. Open the gate on your right: it links the upper and lower Burg so you can get back to the bonfire quickly.

2. Griggs and the alley

  • Griggs of Vinheim, a sorcerer, is locked in a house near the well. You need the Residence Key (the Undead Burg merchant sells it). Free him and he moves to Firelink Shrine, where he sells sorceries.
  • The alley past him is an ambush. Hollows with torches come from both sides, and thieves who throw knives hide in doorways. Back up and pull them one at a time.
  • Undead dogs are fast and come in packs. Fight them on stairs, where they line up.

3. The aqueduct shortcut

Before the boss, take the detour down the stairs and up a building’s spiral staircase to the aqueduct. A gate there opens straight to Firelink Shrine. A merchant on the aqueduct sells moss, which cures bleeding and poison, and arrows.

4. The Capra Demon

At the end of the alley is the fog gate of the Capra Demon: a small room, a huge demon and two dogs. It’s one of the hardest early bosses because of how little room you have. Read the Capra Demon boss guide first. It drops the Key to the Depths.

The Depths

The key opens a door in the Lower Burg, near the aqueduct shortcut, that leads down into the sewers.

5. Kitchens and the Butcher

The first rooms are a kitchen. A huge Butcher with a cleaver works there. She’s slow but hits very hard. Fight her in the open, never in a corner.

In a side room, a man in a barrel calls for help: that’s Laurentius of the Great Swamp. Break the barrel to free him. He moves to Firelink Shrine and teaches pyromancy, and he’s the one who upgrades the Pyromancy Flame. Even non-pyromancers can learn from him. See the Pyromancer build.

There’s a bonfire near here. Rest.

6. Rats and slimes

  • Giant rats bite and can poison you. A bigger rat lives in a flooded room; lure it out.
  • Slimes hang from the ceiling and drop on you. Look up before walking under dark spots, and hit them with fire.
  • Toward the bottom, creatures with bulging eyes can curse you with their breath. If the curse meter fills, it kills you and halves your health until it’s cured. Kill them quickly or avoid them.

7. The bottom: the Gaping Dragon

The deepest part of the sewers opens onto a huge chamber. Something enormous is waiting. Read the Gaping Dragon boss guide before you step through the fog. It drops the Blighttown Key, the way to Blighttown (Part 6).

Tips

  • Open both shortcuts. The upper/lower gate and the aqueduct gate turn a long walk into a short one.
  • The Capra Demon is the hard part. If it walls you, level up and upgrade your weapon first.
  • Don’t go chasing every corner of the Depths on your first visit. The sewers loop around; find the bonfire and work outward.
