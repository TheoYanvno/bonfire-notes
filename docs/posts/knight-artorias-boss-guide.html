Boss Guide 23: Knight Artorias

guidebossesdlc

In this post
  1. His attacks
  2. Strategy
  3. After the fight

Knight Artorias was one of Gwyn’s greatest knights, the Abysswalker. When you meet him, the Abyss has taken him over. He fights like a storm: flips, lunges and long combos. Many players think he’s the best fight in the game. Learn his rhythm, and punish him when he powers up.
Where The end of the Royal Wood
Reward Soul of Artorias and souls
Recommended Weapon at +12 to +15, 35+ Vitality, good stamina

His attacks

Attack What it looks like How to avoid it
Flip attack Somersaults into the air and slams down Roll sideways just as he comes down
Lunging thrusts Fast thrusts that cover distance Roll toward him and to the side
Combos Chains of three or more swings Count them; attack after the last
Abyss blast A dark explosion around him Back off when he crouches and glows
Power-up Kneels and gathers dark energy Hit him now to interrupt, or he gets stronger

Strategy

  1. Stay calm and roll a lot. You can’t trade hits with him.
  2. When he kneels to power up, attack. Hitting him stops it. If he finishes, his attacks get stronger.
  3. His attacks can be parried. If you’re good at parrying, it’s the fastest way.
  4. Attack only after a combo ends. One or two hits, then get ready to roll.
  5. Heal far away. He closes distance fast.

After the fight

Keep going toward Oolacile Township (Part 20). Back to the guides hub.
